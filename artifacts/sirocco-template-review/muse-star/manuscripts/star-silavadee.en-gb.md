# STAR by Silavadee — Rooftop Bar & Restaurant, Koh Samui

## Introduction

Perched atop the Silavadee Pool Spa Resort on the Lamai hillside, 'STAR' is a rooftop bar and restaurant — one of the very few rooftop venues on Koh Samui. From the top of the building, Lamai Bay unfolds before you; as the sun goes down, the sea shifts from pink to gold, minute by minute.

STAR's signature is four sunken tables built into an infinity-edged pool. Dining while seated as if immersed in the water, facing the sea, is an experience hard to find elsewhere. Rattan lounge beds with cushions and bar stools complete the setting, with just 42 seats in total keeping the atmosphere intimate.

The menu is Western dinner only: handcrafted cocktails (Espresso Martini, Lychee Martini and more), wine, refillable sparkling wine, set dinner menus and à la carte dishes such as pasta, pizza and homemade plates. A DJ or live saxophone and weekly theme nights round out the evening. This is not a loud party bar — it is closer to a romantic dinner with the sunset as its backdrop — a favourite for unhurried evenings.

## Experience — Who It's For / Things to Note

**A good fit for**
- Couples wanting a romantic sunset dinner: with only four sunken tables, booking is all but essential.
- Anyone wanting a relaxed cocktail facing the sea: bar stools and lounge beds are also available.
- Those wanting a quiet evening with live music: DJ/saxophone sets and weekly events.

**May not suit**
- Only 42 seats — sunset slots on weekends and in peak season can fill up. Booking recommended.
- An open-air venue — if it rains, check on the day (no official rain policy published).
- Guests with children or limited mobility: children and elevator policies are not officially published — contact the venue in advance.
- Dress code is Smart Casual. No gender-specific details published.
