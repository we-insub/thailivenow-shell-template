# HONG's Sky Bar — InterContinental Chiang Mai The Mae Ping

## Introduction

HONG's Sky Bar sits on the top (16th) floor of the InterContinental Chiang Mai The Mae Ping in the Chang Khlan district, not far from the old city. The wide open-air terrace faces a full panorama of the Doi Suthep mountains.

The bar comes alive as the sun starts to set. While the horizon shifts from gold to deep violet, the Doi Suthep ridgeline slowly recedes into the night and the city lights flicker on sixteen floors below. The low lounge chairs keep sightlines open, so you can take in the whole sunset without anything in the way.

The drinks list centres on premium cocktails: international spirits paired with local ingredients such as wild Thai honey, lemongrass and Chiang Mai tea distillates. The food comes straight up from the kitchen of HONG's Chinese Restaurant downstairs — Sichuan, Cantonese and Taiwanese tapas-style bites.

## Why visit

### Time it for sunset

Head up right at the 16:00 opening to secure a seat and watch the whole sunset unfold. From 17:00–19:00, happy hour (Sky Social) offers buy-one-get-one on selected cocktails and wines — ideal for couples or friends out for sunset drinks. DJs play on Friday and Saturday nights.

### A different promotion every day of the week

Thursdays 16:00–21:00 bring Ladies Night (Gin & Glow) with a complimentary Bombay G&T for women; daily 16:00–21:00, the bao bun + drink set goes for THB 550 net (Bao After Dark). If you enjoy cocktails, plan your visit around the weekday with friends or colleagues to keep costs down.

### Easy to book, open to non-guests

Reserve via TableCheck, LINE (@interconchiangmai), phone (+66 52 090 998) or email (dining.icchiangmai@ihg.com); walk-ins are also accepted. You don't need to be a hotel guest, which makes it handy for business entertaining or a relaxed evening meetup. Just don't run late — reservations may be auto-cancelled after 15 minutes.

### Worth reconsidering if

This is a fully open-air space, so it may close when it rains. The official menu PDF is offline (404), making it hard to check the full menu and prices in advance. Pricing is premium, with tax and service charge added to listed prices.

## FAQ

**Q. Do I need a reservation?**
A. Walk-ins are welcome, but sunset hours (17:00–19:00) are better booked. Reserve via TableCheck, LINE, phone or email.

**Q. Can non-hotel guests visit?**
A. Yes. The official FAQ states the restaurants and bars welcome all visitors.

**Q. Is there a dress code?**
A. Smart casual. For men, a shirt with long trousers works well; sleeveless shirts are not allowed. Swimwear is not allowed either. Sneakers, sandals and heels are not officially addressed.

**Q. What happens if it rains?**
A. The bar may close in bad weather or rain. Check the weather on the day.

**Q. Are taxes and service charges included?**
A. Listed prices are subject to 7% tax and 10% service charge. Prices marked "net" (e.g. the bao set at THB 550 net) are inclusive.

**Q. Can I bring outside food or drinks?**
A. Outside food is not allowed. Outside alcohol incurs corkage of THB 1,000++ per bottle.

**Q. Can I see the menu in advance?**
A. The official menu PDF is offline, so the full menu is hard to check in advance. The times and prices of the three promotions are on the official promotions page.
