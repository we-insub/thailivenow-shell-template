# HONG's Sky Bar — InterContinental Chiang Mai The Mae Ping

## Introduction

HONG's Sky Bar is on the top (16th) floor of the InterContinental Chiang Mai The Mae Ping in the Chang Khlan area, not far from the old city. The spacious open-air terrace looks out over a full panorama of the Doi Suthep mountains.

The bar truly comes alive as the sun begins to set. As the horizon turns from gold to deep violet, the Doi Suthep ridgeline slowly melts into the night and the city lights come on one by one, sixteen floors below. The low lounge chairs keep your view clear, so you can enjoy the entire sunset without obstruction.

The bar focuses on premium cocktails — international spirits blended with local ingredients like wild Thai honey, lemongrass and Chiang Mai tea distillates. The food comes straight up from the kitchen of HONG's Chinese Restaurant below: Sichuan, Cantonese and Taiwanese tapas-style plates.

## Why visit

### Go timed for the sunset

Reach by the 4 pm opening and grab a seat — you can watch the entire sunset play out. From 5–7 pm there is happy hour (Sky Social) with buy-one-get-one on select cocktails and wines. Good for couples or friends heading out specifically for sunset drinks. DJs perform on Friday and Saturday nights.

### A different promotion on each day

Thursdays 4–9 pm is Ladies Night (Gin & Glow) with a complimentary Bombay G&T for women; every day 4–9 pm, the bao bun + drink set is THB 550 net (Bao After Dark). If you like cocktails, visit on the right day with friends or colleagues to keep the bill reasonable.

### Easy to book, and non-guests are welcome

Book via TableCheck, LINE (@interconchiangmai), phone (+66 52 090 998) or email (dining.icchiangmai@ihg.com); walk-ins work too. You need not be staying at the hotel, so it suits business dinners or a relaxed evening get-together. Only make sure you are on time — reservations may be auto-cancelled after 15 minutes.

### Think twice if

It is a fully open-air venue, so it may shut when it rains. The official menu PDF is offline (404), so checking the full menu and prices beforehand is difficult. Pricing is premium, with tax and service charge added over the listed prices.

## FAQ

**Q. Is a reservation a must?**
A. Walk-ins are fine, but sunset hours (5–7 pm) are better booked. You can reserve via TableCheck, LINE, phone or email.

**Q. Can non-hotel guests come in?**
A. Yes. The official FAQ says the restaurants and bars welcome all visitors.

**Q. Is there a dress code?**
A. Smart casual. For men, a shirt with long trousers is fine; sleeveless shirts are not allowed. Swimwear is not allowed either. Sneakers, sandals and heels find no official mention.

**Q. What if it rains?**
A. The bar may shut in bad weather or rain. Check the weather on the day of your visit.

**Q. Are taxes and service charges included in the prices?**
A. Listed prices attract 7% tax and 10% service charge. Prices marked "net" (the bao set at THB 550 net) are inclusive.

**Q. Can I carry outside food or liquor?**
A. Outside food is not permitted. Outside liquor carries a corkage of THB 1,000++ per bottle.

**Q. Can I see the menu in advance?**
A. The official menu PDF is offline, so the full menu cannot be checked beforehand. Timings and prices of the three promotions are on the official promotions page.
