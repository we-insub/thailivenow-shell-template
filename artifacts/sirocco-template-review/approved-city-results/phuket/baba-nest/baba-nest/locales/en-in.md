# Baba Nest — Sri Panwa Phuket

## Introduction

Set on the hills of Cape Panwa at the southeastern tip of Phuket, Baba Nest is a reservation-only sunset rooftop inside the Sri Panwa resort. It opens for just three hours a day, 17:00 to 20:00 — not a late-night party bar, but a table booked for the exact moment the sun goes down.

Prior booking is a must. Non-resort guests need to commit to a minimum spend of 3,000 baht (net) per person to confirm a reservation, and this amount is non-refundable. Resort guests pay 1,500 baht (net) per person. In return for the steep entry bar, the deck is ringed by an infinity pool, with islands, the Andaman Sea and the southeastern Phuket peninsula spread out in a full 360 degrees.

The signature pour is the sparkling-wine-based 'BUBBLE ON THE ROOF' cocktail line (990 baht) — glasses like Nest of Phuket and Sunset Bubbles on the Rock are served catching the sunset light. Mexican tapas (488 baht) complete the menu. A 7% VAT and 10% service charge apply over and above all menu prices.

This one is for those who want to see the sunset properly, who are prepared to spend the minimum on a cocktail, and who are comfortable with the strict booking and cancellation terms (100% charge on cancellation or no-show). It is not for those looking to drink late into the night — the place shuts at 20:00, and rain closes it automatically.

## Getting there

Phuket has no urban rail, so there is no train option. Cape Panwa is a hilly pocket set apart to the southeast of Phuket Town, difficult to reach by public transport, and there is no official guidance on this. The official Sri Panwa fact sheet puts Phuket International Airport about 45 minutes away.

Your practical options are a taxi, a ride-hailing app (Grab or Bolt), or the hotel car. There is no official word on Grab, Bolt or shuttles, so do plan your return trip in advance as well. The venue closes at 20:00, so you will be travelling at night.

## The experience, who it's for, and the trade-offs

Time at Baba Nest is fixed: three hours, 17:00 to 20:00. Settle into a deck chair and the first thing you notice is the infinity pool rippling below, with the Andaman Sea — studded with islands — opening in a full circle beyond. As the sun begins to dip, cocktails arrive at every table, and the sparkling-based pours of the 'BUBBLE ON THE ROOF' line catch the sunset light, true to the name.

It suits: those willing to keep a day's schedule free for one sunset scene, those wanting a quiet sunset hour with friends or a partner, those happy to sit long over the view with a cocktail and tapas. The real question to ask yourself is whether the 3,000-baht minimum spend feels worth it.

Do keep these trade-offs in mind. This is not a place to drink into the night — at 20:00 the venue closes and guests head down the hill in pre-booked cars. Rain shuts it automatically. In wet weather, non-guests can shift their minimum spend to Baba Poolclub or Baba Soul Food. Cancellations and no-shows attract a 100% charge, so if the weather is a worry, factor in the closure policy before booking. Cape Panwa is a remote hillside area, difficult to reach by public transport, so it is best to arrange your ride back in advance as well.

## FAQ

**How do I book?**
Advance booking is a must. Call 076-371-008 or email dine@babaphuket.com — there is no online booking page. Non-guests must commit to a 3,000-baht (net) per-person minimum spend to confirm, and it is non-refundable. Your table is held up to 15 minutes past the reservation time.

**What exactly is the minimum spend?**
1,500 baht (net) per person for resort guests, 3,000 baht (net) per person for non-guests. For non-guests, the 3,000 baht is the condition for confirming the booking and is non-refundable. Cancel or fail to turn up and 100% of the amount is charged. In other words, the booking itself commits the money — safest to book once your dates and the weather are settled.

**What happens if it rains?**
As per official policy, the venue closes automatically in rain. Non-guests' committed minimum spend can be transferred to Baba Poolclub or Baba Soul Food. Since this is a place one visits for the sunset, keep this condition in mind before booking on a rainy-season date.

**How late can I stay?**
It is open daily 17:00–20:00 only — a three-hour sunset slot, not a late-night drinking spot. No official last-entry time is published, so arrive early and plan to use the full three hours.

**What do menu prices look like?**
As per the official downloadable menus: the signature 'BUBBLE ON THE ROOF' cocktail line at 990 baht, Mexican tapas at 488 baht (add meat 158), wine by the glass 420–1,400 baht. A 7% VAT and 10% service charge apply over and above all prices.

**How do I get there and back?**
There is no train. Phuket International Airport is about 45 minutes from Sri Panwa as per official guidance — take a taxi, Grab/Bolt, or the hotel car. With no official shuttle or ride-hailing guidance, plan your return ride in advance as well. The venue closes at 20:00, so you will be travelling at night.

**Why is it famous?**
As per the official page: featured by Conde Nast Traveler (April 2018), CNN International Top 2 World's 50 Best Beach Bars, CNNgo top 3, and Beach Tomato UK best views. The official selling point is the view — the infinity pool ringing the rooftop deck, with surrounding islands and the Andaman Sea in a full 360 degrees.
