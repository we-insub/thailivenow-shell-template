# Baba Nest — Sri Panwa Phuket

## Introduction

Set on the hills of Cape Panwa at the southeastern tip of Phuket, Baba Nest is a reservation-only sunset rooftop inside the Sri Panwa resort. It opens for just three hours a day, from 17:00 to 20:00 — not a late-night party bar, but a table booked for the precise moment the sun goes down.

Booking ahead is essential. Non-resort guests must commit to a minimum spend of 3,000 baht (net) per person to confirm a reservation, and that sum is non-refundable. Resort guests pay 1,500 baht (net) per person. In return for the steep threshold, the deck is ringed by an infinity pool, with islands, the Andaman Sea and the southeastern Phuket peninsula spread out in a full 360 degrees.

The signature pour is the sparkling-wine-based 'BUBBLE ON THE ROOF' cocktail line (990 baht) — glasses such as Nest of Phuket and Sunset Bubbles on the Rock arrive catching the sunset light. Mexican tapas (488 baht) complete the menu. A 7% VAT and 10% service charge are added to all menu prices.

This is for those who want to see the sunset properly, who are happy to spend the minimum on a cocktail, and who can accept the strict booking and cancellation terms (a 100% charge on cancellation or no-show). It's not for anyone hoping to drink into the small hours — the doors close at 20:00, and rain shuts the venue automatically.

## Getting there

Phuket has no urban rail, so there's no train to take. Cape Panwa is a hilly area set apart to the southeast of Phuket Town, awkward to reach by public transport, and there's no official guidance on the matter. The official Sri Panwa fact sheet puts Phuket International Airport about 45 minutes away.

Your realistic options are a taxi, a ride-hailing app (Grab or Bolt), or the hotel car. There's no official word on Grab, Bolt or shuttles, so do plan your journey home in advance as well. The venue closes at 20:00, so you'll be travelling at night.

## The experience, who it's for, and the trade-offs

Time at Baba Nest is fixed: three hours, 17:00 to 20:00. Settle onto the deck and the first thing you'll notice is the infinity pool shimmering below, with the Andaman Sea — scattered with islands — opening in a full circle beyond. As the sun begins to dip, cocktails arrive at every table, and the sparkling-based pours of the 'BUBBLE ON THE ROOF' line catch the sunset light, just as the name suggests.

It's a good fit for: anyone happy to keep a day's plans free for a single sunset scene, anyone after a quiet sunset hour with friends or a partner, anyone content to linger over the view with a cocktail and tapas. The real question is whether the 3,000-baht minimum spend feels worth it to you.

Bear these trade-offs in mind. This is no place to drink into the night — at 20:00 the venue closes and guests head down the hill in pre-booked cars. Rain closes it automatically. In wet weather, non-guests can move their minimum spend to Baba Poolclub or Baba Soul Food. Cancellations and no-shows are charged at 100%, so if the weather is a concern, do factor the closure policy in before booking. Cape Panwa is a remote hillside spot, awkward to reach by public transport, so it's wise to arrange your ride home in advance too.

## FAQ

**How do I book?**
Booking ahead is essential. Call 076-371-008 or email dine@babaphuket.com — there is no online booking page. Non-guests must commit to a 3,000-baht (net) per-person minimum spend to confirm, and it's non-refundable. Your table is held until 15 minutes past the reservation time.

**What exactly is the minimum spend?**
1,500 baht (net) per person for resort guests, 3,000 baht (net) per person for non-guests. For non-guests, the 3,000 baht is the condition for confirming the booking and is non-refundable. Cancel or fail to turn up and 100% of the amount is charged. In other words, the booking itself commits the money — safest to book once your dates and the weather are settled.

**What happens if it rains?**
Under official policy the venue closes automatically in rain. Non-guests' committed minimum spend can be transferred to Baba Poolclub or Baba Soul Food. As this is a place you visit for the sunset, do bear this condition in mind before booking on a rainy-season date.

**How late can I stay?**
It's open daily 17:00–20:00 only — a three-hour sunset slot, not a late-night drinking spot. No official last-entry time is published, so arrive early and plan to make the most of the full three hours.

**What do menu prices look like?**
According to the official downloadable menus: the signature 'BUBBLE ON THE ROOF' cocktail line at 990 baht, Mexican tapas at 488 baht (add meat 158), wine by the glass 420–1,400 baht. A 7% VAT and 10% service charge are added to all prices.

**How do I get there and back?**
There's no train. Phuket International Airport is about 45 minutes from Sri Panwa according to official guidance — take a taxi, Grab/Bolt, or the hotel car. With no official shuttle or ride-hailing guidance, plan your return journey in advance too. The venue closes at 20:00, so you'll be travelling at night.

**Why is it famous?**
According to the official page: featured by Conde Nast Traveler (April 2018), CNN International Top 2 World's 50 Best Beach Bars, CNNgo top 3, and Beach Tomato UK best views. The official draw is the view — the infinity pool ringing the rooftop deck, with the surrounding islands and the Andaman Sea in a full 360 degrees.
