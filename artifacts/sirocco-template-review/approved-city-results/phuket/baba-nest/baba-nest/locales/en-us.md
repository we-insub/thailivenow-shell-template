# Baba Nest — Sri Panwa Phuket

## Introduction

Up on the hills of Cape Panwa at the southeastern tip of Phuket, Baba Nest is a reservation-only sunset rooftop inside the Sri Panwa resort. It's open just three hours a day, 5:00 to 8:00 PM — not a late-night party bar, but a seat you book for the exact moment the sun goes down.

Reservations are required. Non-resort guests have to commit to a minimum spend of 3,000 baht (net) per person to lock in a booking, and that amount is non-refundable. Resort guests pay 1,500 baht (net) per person. For that steep threshold you get a deck wrapped by an infinity pool, with islands, the Andaman Sea, and the southeastern Phuket peninsula opening up in a full 360 degrees.

The signature pour is the sparkling-wine-based 'BUBBLE ON THE ROOF' cocktail line (990 baht) — drinks like Nest of Phuket and Sunset Bubbles on the Rock show up catching the sunset light. Mexican tapas (488 baht) round out the menu. A 7% VAT and 10% service charge get added to all menu prices.

This is for people who want to see the sunset right, who are fine spending the minimum on a cocktail, and who can live with the strict booking and cancellation terms (100% charge on cancellation or no-show). It's not for anyone looking to drink into the night — doors close at 8:00 PM, and rain shuts the place down automatically.

## Getting there

Phuket has no urban rail, so there's no train to take. Cape Panwa is a hilly area off to the southeast of Phuket Town, tough to reach by public transit, and there's no official guidance on it. The official Sri Panwa fact sheet puts Phuket International Airport about 45 minutes away.

Your realistic options are a taxi, a ride-hailing app (Grab or Bolt), or the hotel car. There's no official info on Grab, Bolt, or shuttles, so line up your ride home ahead of time too. The venue closes at 8:00 PM, so you'll be traveling at night.

## The experience, who it's for, and the trade-offs

Time at Baba Nest is fixed: three hours, 5:00 to 8:00 PM. Grab a seat on the deck and the first thing you notice is the infinity pool rippling below, with the Andaman Sea — dotted with islands — opening in a full circle beyond. As the sun starts to drop, cocktails land on every table, and the sparkling-based pours of the 'BUBBLE ON THE ROOF' line catch the sunset light, just like the name says.

It's a great fit for: anyone willing to keep a day's schedule open for one sunset scene, anyone wanting a quiet sunset hour with friends or a partner, anyone happy to linger over the view with a cocktail and tapas. The real question is whether the 3,000-baht minimum spend feels worth it to you.

Know the trade-offs going in. This isn't a place to drink into the night — at 8:00 PM the venue closes and guests head down the hill in cars they booked ahead. Rain closes it automatically. In wet weather, non-guests can move their minimum spend over to Baba Poolclub or Baba Soul Food. Cancellations and no-shows get charged 100%, so if weather's a worry, keep the closure policy in mind before booking. Cape Panwa is a remote hillside area, hard to reach by public transit, so it's smart to sort out your ride home in advance too.

## FAQ

**How do I book?**
Book ahead — it's required. Call 076-371-008 or email dine@babaphuket.com; there's no online booking page. Non-guests have to commit to a 3,000-baht (net) per-person minimum spend to confirm, and it's non-refundable. Your table is held until 15 minutes past the reservation time.

**What exactly is the minimum spend?**
1,500 baht (net) per person for resort guests, 3,000 baht (net) per person for non-guests. For non-guests, the 3,000 baht is what confirms the booking, and it's non-refundable. Cancel or no-show and 100% of that amount gets charged. Basically, the reservation itself puts money on the line — safest to book once your dates and the weather are set.

**What happens if it rains?**
Per official policy, the venue closes automatically when it rains. Non-guests' committed minimum spend can be transferred to Baba Poolclub or Baba Soul Food. Since this is a place you go for the sunset, keep this condition in mind before booking during rainy season.

**How late can I stay?**
It's open daily 5:00–8:00 PM only — a three-hour sunset window, not a late-night drinking spot. No official last-entry time is published, so get there early and plan to use the full three hours.

**What do menu prices look like?**
Per the official downloadable menus: the signature 'BUBBLE ON THE ROOF' cocktail line at 990 baht, Mexican tapas at 488 baht (add meat 158), wine by the glass 420–1,400 baht. A 7% VAT and 10% service charge get added to all prices.

**How do I get there and back?**
There's no train. Phuket International Airport is about 45 minutes from Sri Panwa per official guidance — take a taxi, Grab/Bolt, or the hotel car. With no official shuttle or ride-hailing info, plan your return ride ahead of time too. The venue closes at 8:00 PM, so you'll be traveling at night.

**Why is it famous?**
Per the official page: listed by Conde Nast Traveler (April 2018), CNN International Top 2 World's 50 Best Beach Bars, CNNgo top 3, and Beach Tomato UK best views. The official selling point is the view — the infinity pool wrapping the rooftop deck, with the surrounding islands and the Andaman Sea in a full 360 degrees.
