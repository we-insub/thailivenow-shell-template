# Twilight Sky Restaurant & Bar

Twilight Sky Restaurant & Bar is located inside The SIS Kata Resort, on the hillside above Kata Beach. The official name describes it well — "Sunset Terrace". From the terrace, the curve of Kata Beach and the Andaman Sea lie below you, and if you are seated as the sun begins to set, you can watch the sky turn from pink to violet with a cocktail in hand.

The distinctive feature here is the line-up of promotions through the day. "Happy in Style" runs from 11:00 to 01:00 (selected cocktails 149 baht, Chang beer 119 baht, until 2026-10-31), an afternoon tea set is served daily from 11:00–16:00 (499 baht), "Bubble Sunset" is on from 18:00–20:00 (prosecco and cold-cut set, 1,999 baht for two), and Thursday is steak night (18:00–21:30, with a live trio band). Visit at different hours on the same day and a different programme awaits. The food menu carries the line "THE PRICE YOU SEE IS THE PRICE YOU PAY" — the printed price is the price you pay.

The concept combines Thai street-food flavours with hot-stone cooking, presented as Asian–European fusion, so even those in your group who are not familiar with Thai food will find it easy to order. There is live music on Thursdays, and a DJ pool party twice a month lends the evening a different mood.

## Experience · Who It's For & What to Know

### A good fit for

Travellers who like choosing a promotion to suit the hour, and those who wish to combine dinner with a sunset over Kata. Thursday evenings bring the trio band and steak night, and the twice-monthly DJ pool party suits adult groups looking for an evening with music. The fusion menu keeps ordering simple even if someone in your group is trying Thai flavours for the first time.

### Worth noting

The venue is on a hill, so taking a Grab, Bolt or taxi up from Kata Beach is more convenient than walking. With promotions running late (Happy in Style continues until 01:00), do plan your return transport in advance.

The venue's own operating hours are not published officially. If you wish to visit outside the promotion hours, do call on the day (+66 76 609555) to confirm it is open.

There is no separate official policy on children for the restaurant. The resort carries Adult Only branding, but that cannot be assumed to extend to Twilight Sky, so if you are planning to visit with children, please confirm directly by phone before your visit.

## FAQ

**Q. Can I visit if I am not staying at the resort?**

A. The official page mentions no guests-only restriction. Public booking channels — phone, email, Messenger and LINE — are listed, so you may visit without staying there.

**Q. How do I make a reservation?**

A. By phone (+66 76 609555), email (yourstay@thesiskata.com; for the restaurant, fb@thesiskata.com), Facebook Messenger or LINE. There is no dedicated online reservation form for the restaurant. Once your date and group size are finalised, contact them through whichever channel is convenient.

**Q. Are taxes and service charges added to the listed prices?**

A. The food menu is pay-as-printed. The official menu PDF states "THE PRICE YOU SEE IS THE PRICE YOU PAY". Whether the promotional drinks include tax and service charge is not stated officially, so please ask the staff when ordering.

**Q. When should I go to catch the sunset?**

A. Aim for the 18:00–20:00 "Bubble Sunset" slot. The prosecco and cold-cut set for two is 1,999 baht — a pleasant way to begin the evening watching the sun set from the terrace.

**Q. What is the Thursday steak night?**

A. A weekly steak night held every Thursday from 18:00–21:30, with live music from a trio band alongside dinner. If Thursday suits your itinerary, it is a good evening to visit.

**Q. Do they serve afternoon tea?**

A. Yes — an afternoon tea set (499 baht) is served daily from 11:00–16:00. If you are in Kata during the day, it is a relaxed way to spend an hour on the terrace.
