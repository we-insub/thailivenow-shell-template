# Twilight Sky Restaurant & Bar

Twilight Sky Restaurant & Bar is set inside The SIS Kata Resort, on the hillside above Kata Beach. The official name gives the game away — "Sunset Terrace". From the terrace you look down over the sweep of Kata Beach and the Andaman Sea, and if you're seated as the sun starts to drop, you can watch the sky turn from pink to violet with a cocktail in hand.

The draw here is the rolling programme of promotions through the day. "Happy in Style" runs 11:00 to 01:00 (selected cocktails 149 baht, Chang beer 119 baht, until 2026-10-31), there's an afternoon tea set daily from 11:00–16:00 (499 baht), "Bubble Sunset" from 18:00–20:00 (prosecco and cold-cut set, 1,999 baht for two), and Thursday steak night (18:00–21:30, with a live trio band). Visit at different times of day and there's a different line-up waiting. The food menu carries the line "THE PRICE YOU SEE IS THE PRICE YOU PAY" — what you see is what you pay.

The concept pairs Thai street-food flavours with hot-stone cooking, done as Asian–European fusion, so even companions unfamiliar with Thai food will find choosing straightforward. Thursdays bring live music, and a DJ pool party twice a month gives the evening a different feel.

## Experience · Who It's For & What to Know

### A good fit for

Travellers who like matching a promotion to the time of day, and anyone wanting to sort dinner while watching the sun set over Kata. Thursday evenings bring the trio band and steak night, and the twice-monthly DJ pool party suits adult groups after an evening with music. The fusion menu keeps things simple even if someone in your party is new to Thai food.

### Worth knowing

It's up a hill, so a Grab, Bolt or taxi from Kata Beach is easier than walking up. With promotions running late (Happy in Style continues until 01:00), it's worth sorting your return journey in advance.

The venue's own opening hours aren't published officially. If you'd like to visit outside promotion hours, ring on the day (+66 76 609555) to confirm it's open.

There's no separate official policy on children for the restaurant. The resort is branded Adult Only, but that can't be taken to apply to Twilight Sky, so if you're planning to bring children, enquire by phone beforehand.

## FAQ

**Q. Can I visit if I'm not staying at the resort?**

A. The official page mentions no guests-only restriction. Public booking channels — phone, email, Messenger and LINE — are advertised, so you can visit without staying there.

**Q. How do I book?**

A. By phone (+66 76 609555), email (yourstay@thesiskata.com; for the restaurant, fb@thesiskata.com), Facebook Messenger or LINE. There's no dedicated online booking form for the restaurant. Once your date and party size are fixed, get in touch via whichever channel suits you.

**Q. Are taxes and service charges added to the listed prices?**

A. The food menu is pay-what's-printed. The official menu PDF states "THE PRICE YOU SEE IS THE PRICE YOU PAY". Whether promotional drinks include tax and service isn't stated officially, so ask the staff when ordering.

**Q. When should I go to catch the sunset?**

A. Aim for the 18:00–20:00 "Bubble Sunset" slot. The prosecco and cold-cut set is 1,999 baht for two — a fine way to begin the evening watching the sun go down from the terrace.

**Q. What is Thursday steak night?**

A. A weekly steak night every Thursday from 18:00–21:30, with live music from a trio band alongside dinner. If Thursday fits your plans, it's a good evening to visit.

**Q. Do they do afternoon tea?**

A. Yes — an afternoon tea set (499 baht) is served daily from 11:00–16:00. If you're in Kata during the day, it's a relaxed way to spend an hour on the terrace.
