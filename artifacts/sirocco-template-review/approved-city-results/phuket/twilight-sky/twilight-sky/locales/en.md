# Twilight Sky Restaurant & Bar

Twilight Sky Restaurant & Bar sits inside The SIS Kata Resort, on the hillside above Kata Beach. Its official name says it all — "Sunset Terrace". From the terrace you look out over the curve of Kata Beach and the Andaman Sea, and if you take your seat as the sun starts to dip, you can watch the sky shift from pink to violet with a cocktail in hand.

What sets this place apart is that there's a different promotion for every part of the day. "Happy in Style" runs from 11:00 all the way to 01:00 (selected cocktails 149 baht, Chang beer 119 baht, until 2026-10-31), there's an afternoon tea set every day from 11:00–16:00 (499 baht), "Bubble Sunset" from 18:00–20:00 (prosecco and cold-cut set, 1,999 baht for two), and Thursday steak night (18:00–21:30, with a live trio band). Come at different hours on the same day and you'll find a different line-up waiting. The food menu carries the line "THE PRICE YOU SEE IS THE PRICE YOU PAY" — the price printed is the price you pay.

The concept pairs Thai street-food flavours with hot-stone cooking, reworked as Asian–European fusion, so even companions who aren't familiar with Thai food will have no trouble choosing. Live music plays on Thursdays, and a DJ pool party happens twice a month, changing the feel of the evening.

## Experience · Who It's For & What to Know

### A good fit for

Travellers who enjoy picking a promotion to match the hour, and anyone who wants to sort out dinner while watching the sunset over Kata. Thursday evenings bring the trio band and steak night, and the twice-monthly DJ pool party suits adult groups after an evening with music. The fusion menu keeps things easy even if someone in your party is new to Thai food.

### Worth knowing

It's on a hill, so a Grab, Bolt or taxi up from Kata Beach is easier than walking. With promotions running late (Happy in Style goes until 01:00), think about your ride back in advance.

The venue's own opening hours aren't published officially. If you'd like to visit outside promotion hours, call on the day (+66 76 609555) to confirm it's open.

There's no separate official policy on children for the restaurant. The resort carries Adult Only branding, but that can't be assumed to apply to Twilight Sky, so if you're planning to bring kids, call ahead and check directly.

## FAQ

**Q. Can I visit if I'm not staying at the resort?**

A. The official page states no guests-only restriction. Booking channels open to the public — phone, email, Messenger and LINE — are published, so you can visit without staying there.

**Q. How do I book?**

A. By phone (+66 76 609555), email (yourstay@thesiskata.com; for the restaurant, fb@thesiskata.com), Facebook Messenger or LINE. There is no dedicated online reservation form for the restaurant. Once your date and party size are set, get in touch through whichever channel suits you.

**Q. Are taxes and service charges added to the listed prices?**

A. The food menu is pay-what's-printed. The official menu PDF states "THE PRICE YOU SEE IS THE PRICE YOU PAY". Whether the promotional drinks include tax and service charge isn't stated officially, so ask the staff when you order.

**Q. When should I go to catch the sunset?**

A. Aim for the 18:00–20:00 "Bubble Sunset" slot. The prosecco and cold-cut set is 1,999 baht for two — a good way to start the evening watching the sun go down from the terrace.

**Q. What is Thursday steak night?**

A. A weekly steak night every Thursday from 18:00–21:30, with live music from a trio band alongside dinner. If Thursday fits your schedule, it's a good time to visit.

**Q. Do they serve afternoon tea?**

A. Yes — an afternoon tea set (499 baht) runs daily from 11:00–16:00. If you're in Kata during the day, it's a relaxed way to spend an hour on the terrace.
