# Twilight Sky Restaurant & Bar

Twilight Sky Restaurant & Bar is inside The SIS Kata Resort, up on the hill above Kata Beach. The official name tells you exactly what you're getting — "Sunset Terrace." From the terrace you get a wide view over the curve of Kata Beach and the Andaman Sea, and if you grab a table as the sun starts going down, you can watch the sky go from pink to purple with a cocktail in hand.

The thing that makes this place different is that there's a different promo running at pretty much every hour of the day. "Happy in Style" goes from 11:00 AM all the way to 1:00 AM (select cocktails 149 baht, Chang beer 119 baht, through 2026-10-31), afternoon tea is served daily 11:00–16:00 (499 baht), "Bubble Sunset" runs 18:00–20:00 (prosecco and cold-cut set for two, 1,999 baht), and Thursday is steak night (18:00–21:30, with a live trio band). Show up at different times on the same day and there's a different lineup waiting. The food menu says it straight: "THE PRICE YOU SEE IS THE PRICE YOU PAY" — the price listed is the price you pay.

The menu concept mixes Thai street-food flavors with hot-stone cooking, done up as Asian–European fusion, so even if someone in your group isn't into Thai food, they'll have no trouble finding something. There's live music on Thursdays, and a DJ pool party twice a month that changes up the vibe for the evening.

## Experience · Who It's For & What to Know

### A good fit for

Travelers who like picking a promo to match the time of day, and anyone who wants to take care of dinner while watching the sun set over Kata. Thursday evenings bring the trio band and steak night, and the twice-a-month DJ pool party is a fit for adult groups looking for an evening with music. The fusion menu keeps things easy even if someone in your group is new to Thai food.

### Good to know

It's up on a hill, so grabbing a Grab, Bolt, or taxi from Kata Beach beats walking up. With promos running late (Happy in Style goes until 1:00 AM), line up your ride back ahead of time.

The venue's own hours aren't posted officially. If you want to visit outside promo hours, call ahead that day (+66 76 609555) to make sure it's open.

There's no separate official policy on kids for the restaurant. The resort is branded Adult Only, but that can't be assumed to apply to Twilight Sky, so if you're bringing children, call and check directly before you go.

## FAQ

**Q. Can I visit if I'm not staying at the resort?**

A. The official page lists no guests-only restriction. Public booking channels — phone, email, Messenger, and LINE — are advertised, so you can visit without staying there.

**Q. How do I make a reservation?**

A. By phone (+66 76 609555), email (yourstay@thesiskata.com; for the restaurant, fb@thesiskata.com), Facebook Messenger, or LINE. There's no dedicated online reservation form for the restaurant. Once your date and headcount are set, reach out through whichever channel works for you.

**Q. Are taxes and service charges added on top of the listed prices?**

A. The food menu is pay-what's-listed. The official menu PDF says "THE PRICE YOU SEE IS THE PRICE YOU PAY." Whether promo drinks include tax and service isn't stated officially, so ask your server when you order.

**Q. When should I go to catch the sunset?**

A. Time it for the 18:00–20:00 "Bubble Sunset" window. The prosecco and cold-cut set for two is 1,999 baht — a solid way to kick off the evening watching the sun drop from the terrace.

**Q. What's Thursday steak night?**

A. A weekly steak night every Thursday from 18:00–21:30, with live music from a trio band alongside dinner. If Thursday works with your schedule, it's a great night to go.

**Q. Do they serve afternoon tea?**

A. Yes — an afternoon tea set (499 baht) is served daily from 11:00–16:00. If you're in Kata during the day, it's a relaxed way to spend an hour on the terrace.
