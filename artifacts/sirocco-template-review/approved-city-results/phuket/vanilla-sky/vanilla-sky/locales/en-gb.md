# Vanilla Sky Bar & Gastro Pub

Up on the headland at the northern end of Kamala Beach you'll find Vanilla Sky Bar & Gastro Pub, the rooftop bar and gastro pub of Cape Sienna Phuket Gourmet Hotel & Villas. The official line leads with sea views, creative craft cocktails and modern gastro-pub cooking — and the venue's own suggested order is the signature 'Smoky Cinnamon' or a draught beer at sunset, with dishes from the Argentine charcoal-grill BBQ zone on the side.

Cocktails are the main event here: this is less a dinner destination, more a place to settle in over drinks and light bites as the sun goes down. Ideal if you love a good cocktail and want to watch the whole golden hour from your seat. The gin list runs to 69, and the choice is broad — craft cocktails through to craft beer. Anyone after a quiet sit-down meal, though, may find the bar buzz a bit much.

## Public Transport · Grab / Bolt

There's no BTS or MRT in Phuket, so it's a taxi or a Grab/Bolt ride out to the Kamala headland. The official brochure puts the hotel limousine at about 40 minutes from Phuket International Airport, and parking is free, so a hire car works too.

No official word on Grab/Bolt fares or late-night rides home, so if you're planning a late one, sort your transport in advance.

## The Experience · Who It's For · What's Tricky

Get there for the 5 pm opening and you catch the whole pre-sunset show from your seat, sea and sky swapping colours. The menu leads with its 69 gins across categories like Special Creations, Sky Bar Craft Cocktails and Grand Supersized — half the fun is choosing. The Smoky Cinnamon is the one the official page names, so on a first visit, start there.

Hungry? The official steer is to pair drinks with the Argentine charcoal-grill BBQ zone. The menu lists gastro-pub snacks — smoked duck, octopus, steak, house-cut chips — at around 70–170 THB, cocktails at 330–430 THB. Treat prices as a guide only: the menu PDF's upload date is unverified, so check the menu on the day. No tax or service charge is shown on the menu, so ask the staff whether it's added when you pay.

This is a several-cocktails-and-a-long-evening kind of place. If you're properly hungry, look at the hotel's other dining options too. Children's, dress code and smoking policies aren't published on the official channels, so families or anyone fussy about dress should check by email first.

## FAQ

**Q. How do I book?**
A. By email to rsvn@capesienna.com. There's no official online booking page. Once your date is set, email ahead.

**Q. When should I go?**
A. Open daily 17:00–24:00. The house recommendation is the signature cocktail with BBQ-zone dishes at sunset — so for the full colour-changing-sky show from your seat, be there for the 5 pm opening.

**Q. What should I drink?**
A. The official page names the 'Smoky Cinnamon' (360 THB). The gin list hits 69, and the range runs from craft cocktails to craft beer. No tax or service charge is shown on the menu, so ask when you pay — and treat prices as a guide until you've seen the day's menu.

**Q. Can I come if I'm not staying at the hotel?**
