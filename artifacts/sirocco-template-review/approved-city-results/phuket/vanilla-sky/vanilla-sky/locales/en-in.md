# Vanilla Sky Bar & Gastro Pub

Vanilla Sky Bar & Gastro Pub is the rooftop bar and gastro pub of Cape Sienna Phuket Gourmet Hotel & Villas, set on the headland at the northern end of Kamala Beach. The official introduction leads with sea views, creative craft cocktails and modern gastro-pub cooking. The venue's own recommendation is a signature 'Smoky Cinnamon' cocktail — or a draught beer — at sunset, with dishes from the Argentine charcoal-grill BBQ zone on the side.

Do note this is a cocktail-first place: it is less about a full dinner and more about lingering over drinks and light bites as the sun goes down. It will suit cocktail lovers who like to settle in for the sunset hour. The gin menu runs to 69 labels, and the selection is wide — craft cocktails to craft beer. That said, anyone looking for a quiet, proper sit-down dinner may find the bar atmosphere a mismatch.

## Public Transport · Grab / Bolt

Phuket has no BTS or MRT, so you reach the Kamala headland by taxi or a Grab/Bolt ride. Per the official brochure, the hotel limousine takes about 40 minutes from Phuket International Airport, and free parking is available, so a rental car works too.

Grab/Bolt fares and late-night return options are not covered in any official notice, so if you plan to stay on late, please fix your transport in advance.

## The Experience · Who It's For · What's Tricky

Time your arrival for the 5 pm opening and you can watch the whole pre-sunset colour show — sea and sky trading shades — from your seat. The menu leads with its 69 gins, grouped into categories like Special Creations, Sky Bar Craft Cocktails and Grand Supersized, so browsing it is part of the fun. The Smoky Cinnamon is named on the official page itself, so on a first visit, that is the one to start with.

If you are feeling hungry, the official suggestion is to pair your drinks with the Argentine charcoal-grill BBQ zone. The menu lists gastro-pub snacks — smoked duck, octopus, steak, house-cut chips — at around 70–170 THB, and cocktails at 330–430 THB. Please treat prices as indicative: the menu PDF's upload date is unverified, so do check the menu on the day. Taxes and service charge are not marked on the menu, so please ask the staff whether they are added at billing.

This is a place for evenings built around several cocktails and a long sit. If you are genuinely hungry, do consider the hotel's other dining options as well. Children, dress code and smoking policies are not published on the official channels, so families, or anyone particular about dress, should please check by email beforehand.

## FAQ

**Q. How do I make a reservation?**
A. Reservations are taken by email at rsvn@capesienna.com. There is no official online booking page. Once your date is fixed, please send the email in advance.

**Q. When is the best time to go?**
A. It is open daily 17:00–24:00. The house recommendation is the signature cocktail with BBQ-zone dishes at sunset, so if you want the full colour-changing-sky show from your seat, please be there for the 5 pm opening.

**Q. What should I order to drink?**
A. The signature named on the official page is the 'Smoky Cinnamon' (360 THB). The gin list runs to 69 labels, and the range goes from craft cocktails to craft beer. Taxes and service charge are not marked on the menu, so please ask at billing — and treat prices as indicative until you have seen the day's menu.

**Q. Can I visit if I am not staying at the hotel?**
A. It operates openly as hotel dining, with no non-guest restrictions stated. There is no explicit welcome message either, so for a large group or a specific table, please email ahead.
