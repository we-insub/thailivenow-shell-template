# Vanilla Sky Bar & Gastro Pub

On the headland at the northern end of Kamala Beach sits Vanilla Sky Bar & Gastro Pub, the rooftop bar and gastro pub of Cape Sienna Phuket Gourmet Hotel & Villas. The official pitch leads with sea views, creative craft cocktails and modern gastro-pub cooking. The way the venue itself recommends: a signature 'Smoky Cinnamon' cocktail or a draught beer as the sun goes down, paired with dishes from the Argentine charcoal-grill BBQ zone.

This is a cocktail-first spot — less somewhere you go for a proper dinner, more somewhere to linger over drinks and light bites at sunset. It suits cocktail lovers who want to settle in for the golden hour. With 69 gins on the menu, the gin line-up is serious, and the choice runs wide from craft cocktails to craft beer. The bar atmosphere, though, may not suit anyone after a quiet sit-down meal.

## The Experience · Who It's For · What's Tricky

Arrive for the 5 pm opening and you get the full show from your seat — the sea and sky trading colours before the sun drops. The menu leads with its 69 gins and is split into categories like Special Creations, Sky Bar Craft Cocktails and Grand Supersized, which makes choosing half the fun. The Smoky Cinnamon is named on the official page itself, so if it's your first visit, start there.

If you're hungry, the official recommendation is to pair your drinks with the Argentine charcoal-grill BBQ zone. The menu lists gastro-pub snacks like smoked duck, octopus, steak and house-cut chips at around 70–170 THB, with cocktails at 330–430 THB. Treat the prices as indicative, though — the menu PDF's upload date is unverified, so check the menu on the day. Taxes and service charge aren't marked on the menu, so ask the staff whether they're added at billing.

This is an evening built around settling in with several cocktails. If you're properly hungry, consider the hotel's other dining options alongside.
