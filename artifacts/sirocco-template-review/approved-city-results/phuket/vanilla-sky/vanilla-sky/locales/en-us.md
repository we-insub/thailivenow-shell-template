# Vanilla Sky Bar & Gastro Pub

Vanilla Sky Bar & Gastro Pub is the rooftop bar and gastro pub of Cape Sienna Phuket Gourmet Hotel & Villas, perched on the headland at the north end of Kamala Beach. The official pitch leads with sea views, creative craft cocktails, and modern gastro-pub cooking. The venue's own recommendation: a signature 'Smoky Cinnamon' cocktail or a draft beer at sunset, paired with dishes from the Argentine charcoal-grill BBQ zone.

This is a cocktail-first spot — less a dinner destination, more a place to hang out over drinks and light bites while the sun goes down. It's a great fit if you love cocktails and want to settle in for the whole golden hour. The gin lineup runs to 69 bottles, and the selection is wide, from craft cocktails to craft beer. The bar vibe, though, may not be right if you're looking for a quiet dinner.

## The Experience · Who It's For · What's Tricky

Show up for the 5 pm opening and you get the full pre-sunset color show from your seat — sea and sky trading colors before the sun drops. The menu leads with its 69 gins, organized into categories like Special Creations, Sky Bar Craft Cocktails, and Grand Supersized, so picking is half the fun. The Smoky Cinnamon is the one the official page calls out by name, so on a first visit, start there.

If you're hungry, the official suggestion is to pair your drinks with the Argentine charcoal-grill BBQ zone. The menu lists gastro-pub snacks like smoked duck, octopus, steak, and house-cut chips at around 70–170 THB, with cocktails at 330–430 THB. Treat prices as a guideline, though — the menu PDF's upload date is unverified, so check the menu on the day. Taxes and service charge aren't shown on the menu, so ask the staff whether they're added at checkout.
