# Sizzle Rooftop Restaurant & Bar

Set on the top-floor terrace of Avista Hideaway Phuket Patong – MGallery, high above Patong town, Sizzle Rooftop Restaurant & Bar looks out over the Andaman Sea — the setting for steak and seafood at sunset. The official website leads with the line "PHUKET'S BEST SUNSET SEA VIEWS".

The bar (17:30–23:30) and the restaurant (18:00–23:00, last kitchen order 22:30) share the same terrace on staggered timings. With the bar opening first, an evening can start with a sundowner cocktail and flow naturally into dinner from 18:00. Even when rain is in the forecast, all-weather covered seating is officially listed, so evening plans need not change.

The cooking centres on wagyu, live seafood and the fire of the grill. The official factsheet names the signatures: Tomahawk steak, lobster and tiramisu. The kitchen is led by Chef Alvaro de la Puerta, whose background includes Michelin-starred restaurants, and seafood from the live lobster tank is finished over flame. For a drinks-led evening, the bar — with award-winning mixologists — pours cocktails and tiki cocktails to "laidback tunes" and DJ sets. At its heart this is a dining venue, so it suits a group planning a proper dinner rather than a quiet drink.

Who is it for? A dinner with fellow steak-and-seafood lovers, a celebration or a small get-together all work well. It is geared for group events too — the official factsheet (2025AUG19) lists up to 80 seated on the rooftop terrace, 120 when Tambu alongside is included, and full buyouts from 50 to 120 guests. For a single light cocktail, though, the $$$ price point and dinner-first atmosphere point towards the bar hours (from 17:30–) instead.

All prices are in Thai baht (THB) with 10% service charge and tax extra. The Black Angus Tomahawk MB 5 (1400g) is 6,000 baht on the menu posted to the official site (2025-09-16) — but the menu carries a "MENU IS SUBJECT TO CHANGE" notice, so current pricing is unverified; keep the budget generous. The official site also carries Michelin Guide 2026, Michelin Selected 2026, Travellers' Choice Awards 2026 and Thailand's Favourite Restaurants 2026 listings.

## Getting there: public transport · Grab · Bolt

Phuket has no urban rail like the BTS or MRT, so a taxi, Grab or Bolt booking is the default way to get around. The resort stands on the hillside (Muen Ngern Road) outside central Patong, so walking there is not a realistic option.

- Grab / Bolt: set the destination to "Sizzle Rooftop Restaurant & Bar, Avista Hideaway Phuket Patong" and book.
- Heading back: the bar stays open till 23:30. Late at night, a Grab or Bolt booking is the standard choice, and taxis are available at the resort lobby too.

## The experience · who it's for / what to keep in mind

About an hour before sunset, settle in on the terrace above Patong. As the sky over the Andaman Sea colours, lobster from the live tank and wagyu from the grill arrive at the table while the bar's DJ set plays. As dinner winds down, the sunset spot eases into a night bar, and with service till 23:30 the evening's last drink can be finished from the same table.

**A good fit when:** you want a proper steak-and-seafood dinner with the sunset. Celebrations, small gatherings, group dinners. Covered seating keeps it running when rain is forecast, so the weather is less of a worry.

**Think it over when:** a single light cocktail is the plan — the price level and ambience are dinner-first, so the bar hours (from 17:30–) are the better call. Prices carry 10% service charge + tax extra, and the 6,000-baht Tomahawk comes from the menu posted 2025-09-16, so current pricing is unverified. Dress code, smoking, children and cancellation policies are not published on official channels, so if any of them matter, confirm when booking — info@sizzlerooftopphuket.com or +66 95 834 0453.

A. Yes. The bar runs 17:30–23:30, the restaurant 18:00–23:00, and the last kitchen order is 22:30. With the bar opening first, starting with a cocktail around sunset and moving into dinner from 18:00 works naturally. There are no weekly holidays — it is open every day.

**Q. What happens when it rains?**
A. The official site lists "under all-weather covered seating", so it keeps running under cover when it rains. It does not close for rain; seating shifts to the covered area.
