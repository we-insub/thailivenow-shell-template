# Sizzle Rooftop Restaurant & Bar

Perched on the Patong hillside atop the Avista Hideaway Phuket Patong – MGallery, Sizzle Rooftop Restaurant & Bar is built for one scene: the sun dropping into the Andaman Sea over steak and seafood. The official site's very first line is "PHUKET'S BEST SUNSET SEA VIEWS".

The bar (17:30–23:30) and the restaurant (18:00–23:00, last kitchen order 22:30) share the same terrace on different schedules. Since the bar opens first, an evening that kicks off with a sunset cocktail and flows into dinner from 18:00 unfolds naturally. Even when rain is in the forecast, all-weather covered seating is officially listed, so evening plans can stay as-is.

The kitchen is all about wagyu, live seafood, and the flavor of the grill. The official fact sheet names the signatures: Tomahawk steak, lobster, and tiramisu. The kitchen is led by Chef Alvaro de la Puerta, whose background includes Michelin-starred restaurants, and seafood from the live lobster tank goes straight over open flame. If drinks are the night's mission, the bar — staffed by award-winning mixologists — pours cocktails and tiki cocktails alongside "laidback tunes" and DJ sets. This is a dining-led spot at its core, so it fits a group planning a proper dinner more than a quiet single round.

Who's it for? Dinner with steak-and-seafood lovers, an anniversary, or a small get-together — all good fits. It handles group events too: per the official fact sheet (2025AUG19), up to 80 seated on the rooftop terrace, 120 when Tambu next door is added, and full buyouts from 50 to 120. On the flip side, the price point ($$$) and atmosphere are built around dinner, so for a light single cocktail the bar hours (from 17:30–) are the better bet.

All menu prices are in Thai baht (THB) with a 10% service charge and tax on top. The Black Angus Tomahawk MB 5 (1400g) is 6,000 baht per the menu posted on the official site (2025-09-16) — but the menu carries a "MENU IS SUBJECT TO CHANGE" notice, so current pricing is unverified; budget with room to spare. The official site also lists Michelin Guide 2026, Michelin Selected 2026, Travellers' Choice Awards 2026, and Thailand's Favourite Restaurants 2026.

## Getting there: public transit · Grab · Bolt

Phuket has no BTS/MRT-style urban rail, so calling a taxi, Grab, or Bolt is the default way to get around. The resort sits on the hillside (Muen Ngern Road) outside central Patong, so walking there isn't realistic.

- Grab / Bolt: set the destination to "Sizzle Rooftop Restaurant & Bar, Avista Hideaway Phuket Patong" and book.
- Getting home: the bar runs until 23:30. Late at night, hailing a Grab or Bolt is the norm, and taxis can also be found at the resort lobby.

## The experience · who it's for / what to think about

About an hour before sunset, take a seat on the terrace above Patong. While the sky over the Andaman Sea turns, the table fills with lobster from the live tank and wagyu from the grill, and the bar lays down its DJ set. As dinner winds down, the sunset spot quietly turns into a night bar, and with the bar running until 23:30 the last drink of the evening can finish in the same seat.

**A good fit when:** you want a proper steak-and-seafood dinner with the sunset. Anniversaries, small gatherings, group dinners. Covered seating keeps it going when rain is forecast, so weather is less of a worry.

**Think it over when:** a single light cocktail is the goal — the price level and mood are dinner-first, so aim for bar hours (from 17:30–). Prices are 10% service charge + tax on top, and the 6,000-baht Tomahawk comes from the menu posted 2025-09-16, so current pricing is unverified. Dress code, smoking, children, and cancellation policies aren't published on official channels, so if any of them matter to you, ask at booking time — info@sizzlerooftopphuket.com or +66 95 834 0453.

**Q. Do the bar and restaurant keep different hours?**
A. Yes. The bar runs 17:30–23:30, the restaurant 18:00–23:00, and the last kitchen order is 22:30. Since the bar opens first, starting with a cocktail around sunset and moving into dinner from 18:00 makes for a natural schedule. There are no rest days — it's open every day.

**Q. What happens when it rains?**
A. The official site lists "under all-weather covered seating," so it keeps running under cover when it rains. It doesn't close for rain; seating is directed to the covered area.
