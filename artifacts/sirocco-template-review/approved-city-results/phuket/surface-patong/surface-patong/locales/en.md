# The Surface Restaurant & Bar — La Flora Resort Patong

Head up to the rooftop of a resort that faces Patong Beach head-on. The Surface Restaurant & Bar is the rooftop restaurant and bar of La Flora Resort Patong, where you can start your evening watching a panoramic sunset sweep across Patong Bay and the Andaman Sea. The official line-up is handcrafted cocktails, contemporary cuisine, a curated wine list, and a laid-back seaside mood with ambient music. After a day on the beach, drifting up to the rooftop as dusk falls and opening the night with a cocktail feels like the most natural thing in the world. It's a good fit if you want rooftop atmosphere with dinner at the centre, or if you're looking for an evening spot in central Patong without a trek.

## Map

No coordinates are published on the official channels, so there's no pin on the map. The official address is 39 Taweewong Road, Patong Beach, Phuket 83150, Thailand. Search for "La Flora Resort Patong" or the address in your map app. The resort sits 70 m from Patong Beach and 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road, so it's easy to find in town.

## Public transport · Grab · Bolt

Phuket has no rail, so in Patong town it's basically walking, taxis and ride-hailing apps. The resort is 70 m from Patong Beach, 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road and 8 minutes from Jungceylon, so getting around town is mostly on foot. For longer hops or the airport, Grab and Bolt are convenient; at the airport, use the official AOT metered-taxi counter or the Grab pickup zone. Airport pickup through the resort concierge can be pre-booked too (official FAQ).

## Experience · Who it's for / Things to know

As the sun starts to tilt, cross the resort lobby and head for the rooftop. Patong Bay and the Andaman Sea open up in front of you, and you can open the evening with a cocktail while the sky changes colour. Tables serve contemporary cuisine, the bar side does wine or cocktails — it works both as a dinner-first evening and as a light drinking session. It's 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road but clear of the nightlife-strip noise, so it suits a night when you want a quiet dinner before Patong's evening begins.

Good company: a sunset dinner for two, an evening out with friends, or dinner for travellers staying in Patong town. It fits anyone who wants rooftop atmosphere alongside their food.

A few things worth knowing. Hours, menu and prices aren't published on the current official page, so it's better to confirm by phone or email than to drop in on impulse. If you want a sunset-time table, ask ahead — and check dress code and rainy-day arrangements at the same time. All the official page promises is ambient background music; there's no live-music or DJ schedule. If you're hoping for a musical night, call ahead and ask whether anything is on.

## FAQ

**How do I book?**
By phone, email or the official social channels. There's no dedicated online booking page for The Surface on the official site. Phone +66 (0) 76 344 241, email info@laflorapatong.com, or official Line · Facebook · Instagram (@laflorapatong).

**What are the opening hours?**
The current official page doesn't list hours. An older version of the page showed 17:30–midnight but that's been removed, so as of 2026-10-13 a quick call before you go is the reliable move.

**Can I see the menu and prices in advance?**
Menu names and prices aren't published officially. The official description mentions handcrafted cocktails, contemporary cuisine and curated wines. Ask by email whether they can send the menu ahead.

**Can I visit if I'm not staying at the resort?**
The official page states no guest-only restriction. It's presented as a standalone restaurant and bar inside the resort, so outside visits are structurally possible — but there's no separate admission policy wording, so confirm when you book.

**Can I bring children?**
The whole resort is family-friendly (official FAQ). There's no separate age policy for The Surface itself, so if you're coming with kids, confirm when you book.

**How do I get back?**
In Patong town, walking or calling Grab · Bolt is easiest. For the airport, pre-book the resort concierge pickup, or use the AOT metered-taxi counter or Grab pickup zone at the airport.
