# The Surface Restaurant & Bar — La Flora Resort Patong

Take the lift to the rooftop of a resort that sits directly opposite Patong Beach. The Surface Restaurant & Bar is the rooftop restaurant and bar of La Flora Resort Patong, where you can ease into the evening as a panoramic sunset unfolds over Patong Bay and the Andaman Sea. The official pitch is handcrafted cocktails, contemporary cuisine, a curated wine list and a relaxed seaside atmosphere with ambient music. After a day on the beach, wandering up to the rooftop at dusk and opening the night with a cocktail feels entirely natural. It suits travellers who want their rooftop evening built around dinner, or anyone looking for an evening spot in central Patong without a long journey.

## Map

No coordinates are published on the official channels, so there's no pin on the map. The official address is 39 Taweewong Road, Patong Beach, Phuket 83150, Thailand. Look up "La Flora Resort Patong" or the address in your map app. The resort is 70 metres from Patong Beach and a 2-minute walk from Bangla Road, so it's easy to find in town.

## Public transport · Grab · Bolt

Phuket has no rail, so in Patong town it's essentially walking, taxis and ride-hailing apps. The resort is 70 metres from Patong Beach, 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road and 8 minutes from Jungceylon, so getting about town is mostly on foot. For longer distances or the airport, Grab and Bolt are handy; at the airport, use the official AOT metered-taxi counter or the Grab pickup zone. Airport pickup through the resort concierge can be pre-booked as well (official FAQ).

## The experience · Who it's for / Things to be aware of

As the sun begins to dip, cross the resort lobby and head for the rooftop. Patong Bay and the Andaman Sea stretch out ahead of you, and you can open the evening with a cocktail as the sky changes colour. Tables do contemporary cuisine, the bar side does wine or cocktails — it works equally well as a dinner-first evening or a light drinking session. It's 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road yet set apart from the strip's noise, so it suits an evening when you fancy a quiet dinner before Patong's night gets going.

Good company: a sunset dinner for two, dinner with friends, or dinner for travellers staying in Patong town. It fits anyone who likes their rooftop atmosphere served with proper food.

A few things to be aware of. Hours, menu and prices aren't published on the current official page, so it's worth a phone call or email in advance rather than turning up on spec. If you're after a sunset table, ask ahead — and check the dress code and wet-weather arrangements at the same time. All the official page promises is ambient background music; there's no live-music or DJ schedule. If you're hoping for a night with music, ring ahead and ask whether anything's on.

## FAQs

**How do I book?**
By phone, email or the official social channels. There's no dedicated online booking page for The Surface on the official site. Phone +66 (0) 76 344 241, email info@laflorapatong.com, or official Line · Facebook · Instagram (@laflorapatong).

**What are the opening hours?**
The current official page doesn't list hours. An earlier version of the page showed 17:30–midnight, but that's been removed, so as of 2026-10-13 a quick call before you go is the safe bet.

**Can I see the menu and prices in advance?**
Menu names and prices aren't published officially. The official description mentions handcrafted cocktails, contemporary cuisine and curated wines. Ask by email whether they can send the menu ahead.

**Can I visit if I'm not staying at the resort?**
The official page states no guest-only restriction. It's presented as a standalone restaurant and bar inside the resort, so outside visits are structurally possible — though there's no separate admissions policy, so do confirm when you book.

**Can I bring children?**
The whole resort is family-friendly (official FAQ). There's no separate age policy for The Surface itself, so if you're bringing children, confirm when you book.

**How do I get back?**
In Patong town, walking or hailing Grab · Bolt is easiest. For the airport, pre-book the resort concierge pickup, or use the AOT metered-taxi counter or Grab pickup zone at the airport.
