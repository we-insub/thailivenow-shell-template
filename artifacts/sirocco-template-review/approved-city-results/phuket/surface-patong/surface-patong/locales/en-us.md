# The Surface Restaurant & Bar — La Flora Resort Patong

Head up to the rooftop of a resort facing Patong Beach straight on. The Surface Restaurant & Bar is the rooftop restaurant and bar of La Flora Resort Patong, where you can kick off your evening with a panoramic sunset over Patong Bay and the Andaman Sea. The official pitch: handcrafted cocktails, contemporary cuisine, a curated wine list, and a laid-back seaside vibe with ambient music. After a day on the beach, drifting up to the rooftop at dusk and opening the night with a cocktail just feels right. It's a fit if you want your rooftop night centered on dinner, or if you're looking for an evening spot in central Patong without going far.

## Map

No coordinates are published on the official channels, so there's no pin on the map. The official address is 39 Taweewong Road, Patong Beach, Phuket 83150, Thailand. Search "La Flora Resort Patong" or the address in your map app. The resort is 70 meters from Patong Beach and a 2-minute walk from Bangla Road, so it's easy to find in town.

## Public transit · Grab · Bolt

Phuket has no rail, so in Patong town it's basically walking, taxis, and ride-hailing apps. The resort is 70 meters from Patong Beach, 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road, and 8 minutes from Jungceylon, so getting around town is mostly on foot. For longer trips or the airport, Grab and Bolt are convenient; at the airport, use the official AOT metered-taxi counter or the Grab pickup zone. Airport pickup through the resort concierge can be pre-booked too (official FAQ).

## The experience · Who it's for / Good to know

As the sun starts to dip, cross the resort lobby and head for the rooftop. Patong Bay and the Andaman Sea open up in front of you, and you can open the evening with a cocktail as the sky changes color. Tables serve contemporary cuisine, the bar side does wine or cocktails — it works as a dinner-first evening or a light night out with drinks. It's 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road but away from the strip's noise, so it fits a night when you want a quiet dinner before Patong's evening gets going.

Good company: a sunset dinner for two, dinner with friends, or dinner for travelers staying in Patong town. It's for anyone who wants rooftop atmosphere with real food.

A few things to know. Hours, menu, and prices aren't published on the current official page, so call or email ahead rather than just showing up. If you want a sunset-time table, ask in advance — and check the dress code and rainy-day setup at the same time. The official page only promises ambient background music; there's no live-music or DJ schedule. If you're hoping for a night with music, call ahead and ask if anything's on.

## FAQ

**How do I book?**
By phone, email, or the official social channels. There's no dedicated online booking page for The Surface on the official site. Phone +66 (0) 76 344 241, email info@laflorapatong.com, or official Line · Facebook · Instagram (@laflorapatong).

**What are the hours?**
The current official page doesn't list hours. An older version showed 17:30–midnight, but that's been removed, so as of 2026-10-13, calling ahead is the reliable move.

**Can I see the menu and prices beforehand?**
Menu names and prices aren't published officially. The official description mentions handcrafted cocktails, contemporary cuisine, and curated wines. Ask by email if they can send the menu in advance.

**Can I visit if I'm not staying at the resort?**
The official page states no guest-only restriction. It's presented as a standalone restaurant and bar inside the resort, so outside visits are structurally possible — but there's no separate admission policy, so confirm when you book.

**Can I bring kids?**
The whole resort is family-friendly (official FAQ). There's no separate age policy for The Surface itself, so if you're coming with kids, confirm when you book.

**How do I get back?**
In Patong town, walking or hailing Grab · Bolt is easiest. For the airport, pre-book the resort concierge pickup, or use the AOT metered-taxi counter or Grab pickup zone at the airport.
