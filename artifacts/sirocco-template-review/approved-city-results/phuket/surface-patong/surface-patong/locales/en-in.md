# The Surface Restaurant & Bar — La Flora Resort Patong

Go up to the rooftop of a resort that sits right opposite Patong Beach. The Surface Restaurant & Bar is the rooftop restaurant and bar of La Flora Resort Patong, where you can begin your evening with a panoramic sunset over Patong Bay and the Andaman Sea. As per the official description, expect handcrafted cocktails, contemporary cuisine, a curated wine selection, and a relaxed seaside vibe with ambient music. After a full day at the beach, heading up to the rooftop around dusk and kicking off the night with a cocktail feels like the natural order of things. It works well if you want your rooftop outing centred on dinner, or if you are looking for an evening spot in central Patong without travelling far.

## Map

The official channels have not published coordinates, so there is no pin on the map. The official address is 39 Taweewong Road, Patong Beach, Phuket 83150, Thailand. Search for "La Flora Resort Patong" or the address in your map app. The resort is 70 m from Patong Beach and 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road, so it is easy enough to locate in town.

## Public transport · Grab · Bolt

Phuket has no rail network, so within Patong town it is essentially walking, taxis and ride-hailing apps. The resort itself is 70 m from Patong Beach, 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road and 8 minutes from Jungceylon, so moving around town is mostly done on foot. For longer distances or the airport, Grab and Bolt are handy; at the airport, use the official AOT metered-taxi counter or the Grab pickup zone. Airport pickup through the resort concierge can also be pre-booked (official FAQ).

## Experience · Who it's for / Points to note

When the sun starts dipping, walk through the resort lobby and make for the rooftop. Patong Bay and the Andaman Sea stretch out before you, and you can open the evening with a cocktail as the sky changes colour. The tables are about contemporary cuisine, the bar side about wine or cocktails — so it serves equally well as a dinner-first evening or a relaxed drinks outing. Just 2 minutes on foot from Bangla Road yet away from the strip's noise, it is ideal for a night when you want a quiet dinner before Patong's evening gets going.

Good company: a sunset dinner for couples, a dinner get-together with friends, or dinner for travellers staying in Patong town. It suits anyone who wants to enjoy rooftop atmosphere along with their food.

A few things to keep in mind. Timings, menu and prices are not published on the current official page, so it is better to confirm over phone or email rather than walking in on impulse. If you want a table at sunset hour, ask in advance — and check the dress code and rain-day arrangements at the same time. The official page only promises ambient background music; there is no live-music or DJ schedule. If you are hoping for a night with music, do call ahead and check whether anything is on.

## FAQ

**How do I make a reservation?**
Through phone, email or the official social channels. There is no dedicated online booking page for The Surface on the official site. Phone +66 (0) 76 344 241, email info@laflorapatong.com, or official Line · Facebook · Instagram (@laflorapatong).

**What are the opening hours?**
The current official page does not list opening hours. An earlier version of the page showed 17:30–midnight, but that has been taken down, so as of 2026-10-13, confirming by phone before your visit is the safe option.

**Can I check the menu and prices beforehand?**
Menu names and prices are not officially published. The official description mentions handcrafted cocktails, contemporary cuisine and curated wines. Ask over email whether the menu can be shared in advance.

**Can I visit even if I am not a resort guest?**
The official page mentions no guest-only restriction. It is presented as a standalone restaurant and bar inside the resort, so outside visits are possible in structure — but there is no separate admission policy, so please confirm at the time of booking.

**Can I come with children?**
The entire resort is family-friendly (official FAQ). However, there is no separate age policy for The Surface restaurant itself, so if you are coming with kids, please confirm when booking.

**How do I get back?**
If you are in Patong town, walking or hailing Grab · Bolt is the easiest. For the airport, pre-book the resort concierge pickup, or use the AOT metered-taxi counter or Grab pickup zone at the airport.
