# STAR by Silavadee — Koh Samui Rooftop Bar & Restaurant

## Introduction

Right on top of the Silavadee Pool Spa Resort, above Lamai Bay, you'll find STAR — a rooftop bar and restaurant. Its trademark is four sunken tables set inside the infinity pool: dinner from a seat that feels half-submerged, facing the sea. Hard to find anywhere else. At sunset the bay turns from pink to gold, and this is the closest seat to watch it happen.

Only 42 covers, so it never feels busy. Dinner is Western-style, with handcrafted cocktails and wine. It's made for long, unhurried dinners and conversation — a romantic sunset dinner rather than a loud party bar.

## Why come here

**Sunset over Lamai Bay** — the whole bay lies below the rooftop; the sea changes colour as you eat.

**Sunken tables in the infinity pool** — four tables inside the pool itself. Dinner half-sitting in the water, facing the sea.

## Menu & signatures

Western dinner only. The signatures on the official menu are the Espresso Martini and the Lychee Martini. Put the evening together with a set dinner or à la carte — pasta, pizza, homemade dishes.

## Who it's for

- Couples wanting a romantic sunset dinner
- Anyone fancying a slow cocktail with a sea view
- A quiet evening for conversation

**Worth a thought:** everything is outdoors, so if rain's forecast, ring the resort (+66(0)77-960-555) before setting off. With just 42 covers, sunset in high season and at weekends all but needs a booking.

## When to go

Get there a little before sunset — cocktail in hand as the sun goes down, dinner once it's dark. The most unhurried way to do it.

## Seating

42 covers: four sunken tables in the infinity pool, rattan lounge beds with back cushions, and bar stools.

## Reservations

Book through 'Make a Reservation' on the official site (TableCheck) or the reservations line +66(0)97-939-0555 (8:00–18:00). For sunset, booking ahead is the sensible move.

## Getting in

It's on the rooftop of the Silavadee Pool Spa Resort building.

## Getting there

There's no rail or metro on Koh Samui. Most people hail a Grab or Bolt in the app; it's a car ride to the resort.

## Practical FAQ

**Do I need to book?**
For sunset, book ahead to be safe. It's only 42 covers and high season or weekends can fill up. Book via TableCheck on the official site or the reservations line.

**What should I wear?**
The official dress code is Smart Casual. Think dinner-out clothes: collared shirt and trousers for men, a dress or smart outfit for women.
