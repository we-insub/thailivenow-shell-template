# STAR by Silavadee — Koh Samui Rooftop Bar & Restaurant

## Introduction

On the very top of the Silavadee Pool Spa Resort, above Lamai Bay, sits STAR — a rooftop bar and restaurant. Its calling card: four sunken tables set right inside the infinity pool. You dine half-sitting in the water, the sea in front of you — not an experience you stumble on every day. Around sunset the bay shifts from pink to gold, and this is the closest seat to it.

Just 42 seats, so it never feels crowded. Dinner is Western-style, with handcrafted cocktails and wine. This is a place for long conversations over dinner as the sky changes — closer to a romantic sunset dinner than a party bar.

## Why come here

**Lamai Bay at sunset** — the bay spreads out below the rooftop. You watch the sea change colour as you eat.

**Sunken tables in the infinity pool** — four tables sit inside the pool itself. Dinner from a seat that feels half-submerged, facing the water.

## Menu & signatures

Western dinner only. The official menu's signature cocktails are the Espresso Martini and the Lychee Martini. Build the evening from a set dinner or à la carte — pasta, pizza, homemade dishes.

## Who it's for

- Couples after a romantic sunset dinner
- Anyone who wants an unhurried cocktail facing the sea

**Worth reconsidering:** it's all outdoor seating, so on rainy days call the resort (+66(0)77-960-555) before heading out. With only 42 seats, sunset slots in high season and on weekends effectively require a booking.

## When to go

Arrive a little before sunset — cocktail in hand as the sun drops, then dinner as the sky darkens. That's the most unhurried flow.

## Seating

42 seats: four sunken tables inside the infinity pool, rattan lounge beds with back cushions, and bar stools.

## Reservations

Book via 'Make a Reservation' on the official page (TableCheck) or the reservations line +66(0)97-939-0555 (8:00–18:00). For sunset hours, booking ahead is the safe move.

## Getting there

Koh Samui has no rail or metro. Hailing a Grab or Bolt in the app is the usual way around; you'll ride to the resort by car.

**Do I need a reservation?**
