# STAR by Silavadee — Koh Samui Rooftop Bar & Restaurant

## Introduction

At the very top of the Silavadee Pool Spa Resort, overlooking Lamai Bay, is STAR — a rooftop bar and restaurant. Its signature: four sunken tables built right into the infinity pool. You have dinner from a seat that feels half-submerged, facing the ocean — not something you run into every day. At sunset the bay shifts from pink to gold, and this is the closest seat to it.

Only 42 seats, so it never gets packed. Dinner is Western-style, with handcrafted cocktails and wine. It's built for long conversations over dinner — closer to a romantic sunset dinner than a loud party bar.

## Why come here

**Sunset over Lamai Bay** — the bay stretches out below the rooftop, changing color as you eat.

**Sunken tables in the infinity pool** — four tables inside the pool itself. Dinner from a seat that feels half-submerged, facing the sea.

## Menu & signatures

Western dinner only. The official menu's signature cocktails are the Espresso Martini and the Lychee Martini. Build your evening from a set dinner or à la carte — pasta, pizza, homemade dishes.

## Who it's for

- Couples looking for a romantic sunset dinner
- Anyone wanting a slow cocktail with an ocean view
- A quiet night out for conversation

**Something to consider:** it's all outdoors, so if rain's in the forecast, call the resort (+66(0)77-960-555) before heading over. With just 42 seats, sunset during high season and weekends pretty much requires a reservation.

## When to go

Show up a little before sunset — cocktail in hand as the sun goes down, dinner once it's dark. That's the most relaxed way to do it.

## Seating

42 seats: four sunken tables inside the infinity pool, rattan lounge beds with back cushions, and bar stools.

## Reservations

Book via 'Make a Reservation' on the official website (TableCheck) or the reservations line +66(0)97-939-0555 (8:00–18:00). For sunset, booking ahead is smart.

## Getting in

It's on the rooftop of the Silavadee Pool Spa Resort building.

## Getting there

Koh Samui has no rail or subway. Most people hail a Grab or Bolt in the app; it's a car ride to the resort.

## Practical FAQ

**Do I need a reservation?**
For sunset, book ahead to be safe. Only 42 seats, and high season or weekends can fill up. Book on the official website via TableCheck or the reservations line.

**What's the dress code?**
Officially Smart Casual. Think nice-dinner-out clothes: collared shirt and slacks for men, a dress or polished outfit for women.
