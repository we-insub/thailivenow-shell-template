# STAR by Silavadee — Rooftop Bar & Restaurant, Koh Samui

## Introduction

On the terrace top of the Silavadee Pool Spa Resort, overlooking Lamai Bay, is STAR — a rooftop bar and restaurant. Its speciality is four sunken tables set inside the infinity pool: you dine seated as if in the water itself, facing the sea — an experience you will not easily find elsewhere. At sunset the bay changes from pink to gold, and these are the nearest seats to it.

With only 42 seats it never feels crowded. Dinner is Western-style, accompanied by handcrafted cocktails and wine. It suits long, leisurely dinners and conversation — a romantic sunset dinner rather than a noisy party bar.

## Why visit

**Sunset over Lamai Bay** — the bay lies spread out below the rooftop, its colours shifting as you dine.

**Sunken tables in the infinity pool** — four tables placed inside the pool. Dinner from a seat that feels half-submerged, facing the sea.

## Menu & signatures

Only Western dinner is served. The signature cocktails on the official menu are the Espresso Martini and the Lychee Martini. Compose your evening from a set dinner or à la carte — pasta, pizza, homemade preparations.

## Who will enjoy it

- Couples seeking a romantic sunset dinner
- Those who would like a leisurely cocktail facing the sea
- A quiet evening for conversation

**Please note:** all seating is outdoors, so if rain is forecast, kindly call the resort (+66(0)77-960-555) before leaving. With only 42 seats, a prior reservation is practically necessary for sunset hours during peak season and weekends.

## When to visit

Please arrive a little before sunset — a cocktail in hand as the sun sets, followed by dinner as darkness falls. That is the most relaxed way to enjoy it.

## Seating

42 seats: four sunken tables inside the infinity pool, rattan lounge beds with back cushions, and bar stools.

## Reservations

Please book via 'Make a Reservation' on the official website (TableCheck) or the reservation helpline +66(0)97-939-0555 (8:00–18:00). Advance booking is advisable for sunset hours.

## Entry

It is located on the rooftop of the Silavadee Pool Spa Resort building.

## Reaching there

Koh Samui has no rail or metro. The usual practice is to hail a Grab or Bolt on the app; the resort is reached by car.

## Practical FAQ

**Is a reservation a must?**
For sunset hours it is better to book in advance. There are only 42 seats, and peak season or weekends may fill up. Please book on the official website through TableCheck or the reservation helpline.

**What should I wear?**
The official dress code is Smart Casual. Kindly dress as you would for a pleasant dinner engagement — collared shirt and trousers for gentlemen, a dress or neat attire for ladies.
