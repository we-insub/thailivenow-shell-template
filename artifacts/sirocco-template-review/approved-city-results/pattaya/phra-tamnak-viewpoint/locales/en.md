# Phra Tamnak Mountain Viewpoint

## intro

This is a public viewpoint on the hill south of Pattaya. It is listed in Thailand's official tourism directory as "Phra Tamnak Mountain (Khao Phra Phutthabat)" — a different kind of place from hotel rooftop bars or paid observation decks.

The draw here is the panorama of Pattaya Bay beyond the railing. The crescent-shaped coastline, the city skyline, Bali Hai Pier and the PATTAYA CITY sign on the hill all come into view at once, and on clear days you can see toward Jomtien and the distant islands. The viewpoint updates a sunset-time notice board every day, so many visitors time their visit for the golden hour.

It's a good fit for anyone who wants to photograph the sunset and night lights, first-time visitors who want to get a sense of Pattaya's whole layout, and families or friends who want a viewpoint without spending money. Come up around sunset and watch the city lights switch on from the same spot, from dusk into night.

## facts

- Official name: Phra Tamnak Mountain (Khao Phra Phutthabat) / เขาพระตำหนัก หรือ เขาพระบาท (listed in Thailand's official tourism directory)
- Type: Public outdoor viewpoint (observatory)
- Location: Khao Phra Tamnak hill, between South Pattaya and Jomtien
- Coordinates: 12.9278472, 100.8632145 (OSM node/1044028485)
- Reservation: Not needed (public viewpoint)

## reasons

**Pattaya Bay panorama, from sunset to night lights**
Lean on the railing and look out over Pattaya Bay. You can watch the city lights come on one by one along the crescent coastline, all from the same spot. Check today's sunset time on the notice board and claim your place a little early.

**A hilltop route to walk together**
If it feels like a waste to come up just for the view, walk the hilltop with it. Wat Phra Yai with its golden Big Buddha, the walking paths of Chaloem Phra Kiat Park and the naval monument are all on the same hill. Please dress and behave respectfully in the temple area.

**A good fit for some days, worth a rethink on others**
It's a good choice for days when you want panorama photos or a first-visit orientation of Pattaya. On rainy or windy days, though, the open-air viewpoint is hard to enjoy, and walking up the hill isn't realistic. If you plan to stay late, arrange your ride back in advance.

## access

There is no official transport guide. The viewpoint sits on a hill, so walking up isn't realistic — take a taxi, songthaew (baht bus), Grab or Bolt from central Pattaya. Check your destination against these coordinates: 12.9278472, 100.8632145.

## taxi

In Grab or Bolt, search for "Phra Tamnak Mountain Viewpoint" as your destination. In a taxi, show the driver the English or Thai sentence below.

- English: "Please take me to Phra Tamnak Mountain Viewpoint (Khao Phra Tamnak), Pattaya."
- Thai: "ไปจุดชมวิวเขาพระตำหนัก พัทยาด้วยครับ/ค่ะ" (ครับ = male speaker, ค่ะ = female speaker)

## faq

**Is this a rooftop bar?**
No. It's a public viewpoint listed in Thailand's official tourism directory. It's not a hotel facility, and commercial conditions like reservations don't apply.

**Do I need a reservation?**
No. As a public viewpoint, you can visit without booking ahead.

**When is the best time to go?**
Around sunset is best. The viewpoint updates a sunset-time board every day, so time your visit to it and you'll catch the sun setting over Pattaya Bay followed by the night lights.

**How do I get there and back?**
Go by vehicle — it's on a hill. Take a taxi, songthaew, Grab or Bolt from central Pattaya. Taxis can be hard to find after sunset, so arrange your ride back before you go up for the golden hour.

**What if it rains?**
It's an open-air viewpoint, so rain or strong wind makes viewing difficult. Check the weather forecast on the day and plan accordingly.

**What can I see from the viewpoint?**
The crescent coastline of Pattaya Bay, the city skyline, Bali Hai Pier and the PATTAYA CITY sign on the hill. On clear days you can see toward Jomtien and the distant islands.
