# Phra Tamnak Mountain Viewpoint

## intro

This is a public viewpoint on the hill south of Pattaya. It is listed in Thailand's official tourism directory as "Phra Tamnak Mountain (Khao Phra Phutthabat)" — quite different from hotel rooftop bars or paid observation decks.

The main attraction here is the panorama of Pattaya Bay beyond the railing. The crescent-shaped coastline, the city skyline, Bali Hai Pier and the PATTAYA CITY sign on the hill all come into view together, and on clear days you can see towards Jomtien and the distant islands. The viewpoint updates a sunset-time notice board daily, so many visitors plan their visit around the golden hour.

It suits anyone keen to photograph the sunset and night lights, first-time visitors wanting to get a sense of Pattaya's overall layout, and families or friends looking for a viewpoint that costs nothing. Head up around sunset and watch the city lights come on from the same spot, from dusk into the night.

## facts

- Official name: Phra Tamnak Mountain (Khao Phra Phutthabat) / เขาพระตำหนัก หรือ เขาพระบาท (listed in Thailand's official tourism directory)
- Type: Public outdoor viewpoint (observatory)
- Location: Khao Phra Tamnak hill, between South Pattaya and Jomtien
- Coordinates: 12.9278472, 100.8632145 (OSM node/1044028485)
- Reservation: Not required (public viewpoint)

## reasons

**Pattaya Bay panorama, from sunset to night lights**
Stand at the railing and take in Pattaya Bay. You can watch the city lights switch on one by one along the crescent coastline, all from one place. Check the sunset time on the notice board and get there a little early.

**A hilltop walk to do together**
If coming up only for the view feels incomplete, walk the hilltop as well. Wat Phra Yai with its golden Big Buddha, the walking trails of Chaloem Phra Kiat Park and the naval monument are all on the same hill. Please dress modestly and keep quiet in the temple area.

**Suits some days, worth reconsidering on others**
A good pick for days when you want panorama shots or a first-visit orientation of Pattaya. But on rainy or windy days the open-air viewpoint is no fun, and walking up the hill is not practical. If you plan to linger into the night, sort out your return transport beforehand.

## access

There is no official transport guide. The viewpoint is on a hill, so walking up is not practical — take a taxi, songthaew (baht bus), Grab or Bolt from central Pattaya. Verify your destination with these coordinates: 12.9278472, 100.8632145.

## taxi

In Grab or Bolt, search for "Phra Tamnak Mountain Viewpoint" as the destination. In a taxi, show the driver the English or Thai sentence below.

- English: "Please take me to Phra Tamnak Mountain Viewpoint (Khao Phra Tamnak), Pattaya."
- Thai: "ไปจุดชมวิวเขาพระตำหนัก พัทยาด้วยครับ/ค่ะ" (ครับ = male speaker, ค่ะ = female speaker)

## faq

**Is this a rooftop bar?**
No. It is a public viewpoint listed in Thailand's official tourism directory. It is not a hotel facility, and commercial terms like reservations do not apply.

**Do I need a reservation?**
No. Being a public viewpoint, you can simply turn up.

**When should I go?**
Sunset time is best. The viewpoint refreshes its sunset-time board daily, so plan around it to see the sun go down over Pattaya Bay and the night lights that follow.

**How do I reach there and return?**
Take a vehicle — it is on a hill. Use a taxi, songthaew, Grab or Bolt from central Pattaya. Taxis get scarce after sunset, so fix your return ride before heading up for the golden hour.

**What if it rains?**
It is an open-air viewpoint, so rain or strong wind spoils the visit. Check the day's forecast before you plan.

**What can I see from the viewpoint?**
The crescent coastline of Pattaya Bay, the city skyline, Bali Hai Pier and the PATTAYA CITY sign on the hill. On clear days, Jomtien and the distant islands too.
