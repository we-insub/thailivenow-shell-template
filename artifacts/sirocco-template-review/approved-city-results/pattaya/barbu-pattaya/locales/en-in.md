# BARBÙ — Rooftop Restaurant & Bar on the 24th Floor, Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya

## intro

On the 24th floor of Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya, BARBÙ is the rooftop restaurant and bar the hotel describes as a "Mediterranean retreat in the heart of Pattaya." Bamboo-accented interiors, an open kitchen and a lively bar set the scene for Mediterranean social dining — food meant to be shared.

By day it works as a poolside space beside two pools, with cocktails and light bites. After sunset it turns into a rooftop restaurant for dinner under the stars. The 360-degree view over Pattaya Bay and the city skyline is what the hotel's official fact sheet highlights.

It suits families wanting a rooftop experience with kids. The hotel officially presents family gatherings as a main use case, and since the Skybar Summer Club on the 27th floor is adults-only (18+), families are directed to this 24th-floor space. It also works for anyone who enjoys Mediterranean sharing plates and long tables with friends. With 140 seats, it officially takes group bookings and celebrations.

If you are after an adults-only party atmosphere, that is the 27th floor's role — BARBÙ is a different kind of place.

## reasons

Three reasons people come here.

First, the 24th-floor panorama. Pattaya Bay and the city skyline spread out 360 degrees — sea and city by day, lights by night. Poolside seats suit daytime, dinner tables take over after sunset, the same view in a different mood.

Second, Mediterranean social dining built for sharing. The official online menu lists prices in baht: Greek salad 240, steak tartare 420, margherita pizza 350, antipasto 900 THB. A 10% service charge and 7% VAT are added to listed prices, so factor that into your budget. The setup suits groups gathered around a table, passing dishes.

Third, it is a clearly family-welcoming rooftop. With the 27th-floor skybar restricted to 18+, this is the hotel's official alternative for families — the hotel's own phrase is "a rooftop escape for families and night owls alike." Spend the afternoon poolside with the kids, then enjoy dinner under the stars in the evening.

Good for — families with kids, groups who like Mediterranean sharing food, celebrations and group dinners. Think twice if — you want a late-night party with DJs; that is the Skybar Summer Club on the 27th floor.

## access

Pattaya has no BTS or MRT. Per the hotel's official fact sheet, it is 350 m from Pattaya Beach, opposite Central Marina, 400 m from Terminal 21 Pattaya — in North Pattaya's Second Road (Sai Song) area. Enter the address from the taxi sentences below as your destination in a ride-hailing app.

## faq

**Q. What are the opening hours?**
A. Daily 09:00–23:00 (official). The fact sheet breaks it down: pool 09:00–17:00, dinner & bar 18:00–23:00. Plan poolside time for the afternoon and dinner for the evening.

**Q. Is it ever closed?**
A. Officially "Open Daily" — no closing days.

**Q. How do I book?**
A. There is no online booking link. Contact the hotel at +66 (0) 38 930 600 or WhatsApp +66 93 959 2582. Worth calling ahead for group or celebration bookings.

**Q. What do menu prices look like?**
A. The official online menu lists prices in THB — e.g. Greek salad 240, steak tartare 420, margherita pizza 350, antipasto 900. A 10% service charge and 7% VAT are added to listed prices, so keep that in mind when totaling up.

**Q. Can I bring kids?**
A. Yes — the hotel officially presents family gatherings as a main use case. Since the 27th-floor skybar is 18+, families are directed to this 24th-floor space. Afternoon by the pool with the kids, dinner under the stars in the evening.

**Q. Is it the same place as Skybar Summer Club?**
A. No. BARBÙ is the family-welcoming rooftop restaurant & bar on the 24th floor; Skybar Summer Club is a separate, 18+-only venue on the 27th floor. 27th floor for the party mood, 24th floor for a relaxed meal.
