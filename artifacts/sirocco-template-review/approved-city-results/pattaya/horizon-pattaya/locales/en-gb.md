# Horizon Rooftop Restaurant & Bar

## intro

The very top of Hilton Pattaya, level 34. As the lift doors open, Pattaya Bay unfolds in front of you. An infinity bar sits at the centre, with an indoor dining room on one side and an open-air terrace on the other. Horizon is a rooftop for dining. At sunset the sun sinks beyond the bay, and a live DJ plays every evening. After dark, the city lights and stars appear — which is why the official page describes it as a sunset and stargazing spot.

Ideal if you fancy a proper dinner with a view — an anniversary, a trip with your parents, an evening where the meal is the main event. It also suits winding down the day with a cocktail on the terrace at sunset.

Not the place for a loud party night. This is a fine dining restaurant, and the live DJ is background music.

## reasons

**A 34th-floor panorama.** Pattaya Bay in a single sweeping view. Sunset in the evening, city lights and stars at night.

**Separate spaces for dining and drinks.** Fine dining in the indoor dining room, handcrafted cocktails on the open-air terrace. The infinity bar links the two.

**Signature dishes.** The official page lists a Seafood Tower, Australian Angus Tomahawk Steak, 48-Hour Lamb Shank, and Miso Snow Fish.

**Live DJ every evening.** Background-style sets during dinner hours.

**Suits this kind of trip; think twice for that one.** A strong match for a quiet, upmarket dinner with a view. If you're after a party atmosphere or music late into the night, look elsewhere.

## access

Pattaya has no BTS or MRT. Book a Grab or Bolt (GrabTaxi operates in Pattaya — Pattaya Mail). Set "Hilton Pattaya" as your destination.

For the return journey, book a ride-hailing car again or pick up a taxi from the hotel lobby.
