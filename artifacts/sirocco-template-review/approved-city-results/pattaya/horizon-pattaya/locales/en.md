# Horizon Rooftop Restaurant & Bar

## intro

The very top of Hilton Pattaya, level 34. When the elevator doors open, Pattaya Bay spreads out before you. An infinity bar sits at the center, with an indoor dining room on one side and an open-air terrace on the other. Horizon is a rooftop where you come to eat. At sunset the sun drops beyond the bay, and a live DJ plays every evening. After dark, the city lights and stars come out — which is why the official page introduces this place as a sunset and stargazing spot.

This is for you if you want a proper dinner with a view — an anniversary, a trip with your parents, a night where the meal itself is the plan. It's also right for ending the day with a cocktail on the terrace at sunset.

It's not the place for a loud party night. This is a fine dining restaurant, and the live DJ is background music.

## reasons

**A 34th-floor panorama.** Pattaya Bay at a glance. Sunset in the evening, city lights and stars at night.

**Separate spaces for dining and drinks.** Fine dining in the indoor dining room, handcrafted cocktails on the open-air terrace. The infinity bar connects the two.

**Signature dishes.** The official page lists a Seafood Tower, Australian Angus Tomahawk Steak, 48-Hour Lamb Shank, and Miso Snow Fish.

**Live DJ every evening.** Background-style performances during dinner hours.

**Good for this kind of trip, think twice for that one.** It's a strong match for a quiet, upscale dinner with a view. If you want a party atmosphere or music late into the night, look elsewhere.

## access

Pattaya has no BTS or MRT. Use a ride-hailing app like Grab or Bolt (GrabTaxi operates in Pattaya — Pattaya Mail). Search for "Hilton Pattaya" as the destination.

For the way back, use a ride-hailing app again or catch a taxi at the hotel lobby.

## faq

**Q. When is the best time to go?**
A. Aim for sunset. The official page presents it as a sunset spot, and the live DJ plays every evening. If you're staying for dinner, arrive with time to spare.

A. The official channels don't list opening hours. Third-party sources say daily 16:00 to 01:00. If you're planning dinner, confirm the hours by phone (+66 38 253 000) when you book.

**Q. How do I book?**
A. There's no dedicated booking URL. Contact them by phone (+66 38 253 000) or through the official Facebook page (@Horizonrooftoprestaurantandbar).
