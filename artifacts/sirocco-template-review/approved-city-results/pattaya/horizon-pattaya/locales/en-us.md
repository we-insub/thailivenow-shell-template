# Horizon Rooftop Restaurant & Bar

## intro

The top of Hilton Pattaya, floor 34. When the elevator doors open, Pattaya Bay is right there in front of you. An infinity bar anchors the center, with an indoor dining room on one side and an open-air terrace on the other. Horizon is a rooftop built around dinner. At sunset the sun drops past the bay, and a live DJ plays every night. After dark, the city lights and stars come out — that's why the official page bills it as a sunset and stargazing spot.

This is your place if you want a real dinner with a view — an anniversary, a trip with your parents, a night where the meal is the whole plan. It's also a great way to close out the day with a cocktail on the terrace at sunset.

Don't come expecting a loud party scene. This is a fine dining restaurant, and the live DJ is background music.

## reasons

**A 34th-floor panorama.** All of Pattaya Bay in one look. Sunset in the evening, city lights and stars at night.

**Separate spaces for dinner and drinks.** Fine dining inside, handcrafted cocktails out on the terrace. The infinity bar ties the two together.

**Signature dishes.** The official page lists a Seafood Tower, Australian Angus Tomahawk Steak, 48-Hour Lamb Shank, and Miso Snow Fish.

**Live DJ every night.** Background-style sets during dinner hours.

**Great for this kind of trip; think twice for that one.** A perfect fit for a quiet, upscale dinner with a view. If you're after a party vibe or late-night music, this isn't it.

## access

Pattaya has no BTS or MRT. Grab a Grab or Bolt (GrabTaxi operates in Pattaya — Pattaya Mail). Set "Hilton Pattaya" as your destination.

Heading back, grab another ride-hail or catch a cab at the hotel lobby.
