# Horizon Rooftop Restaurant & Bar

## intro

Right at the top of Hilton Pattaya, on level 34. As the lift doors open, the whole of Pattaya Bay opens up in front of you. There is an infinity bar in the middle, with an indoor dining room on one side and an open-air terrace on the other. Horizon is a rooftop meant for dining. At sunset the sun goes down beyond the bay, and there is a live DJ every evening. Once it gets dark, the city lights and the stars come out — the official page calls it a sunset and stargazing spot for good reason.

Go here if you want a proper dinner with a view — an anniversary, a family trip with your parents, an evening where the meal is the main event. It also works well for winding down the day with a cocktail on the terrace at sunset.

Not the place if you are after a loud party night. This is a fine dining restaurant, and the DJ here is background music.

## reasons

**A 34th-floor panorama.** Pattaya Bay in one sweeping view. Sunset in the evening, city lights and stars at night.

**Separate spaces for food and drinks.** Fine dining in the indoor dining room, handcrafted cocktails out on the terrace. The infinity bar links the two.

**Signature dishes.** The official page lists a Seafood Tower, Australian Angus Tomahawk Steak, 48-Hour Lamb Shank, and Miso Snow Fish.

**Live DJ every evening.** Easy background-style sets during dinner hours.

**Good for this kind of trip, think twice for that one.** A great fit for a quiet, premium dinner with a view. If you want a party vibe or music going late, better to look elsewhere.

## access

Pattaya has no BTS or MRT. Just book a Grab or Bolt (GrabTaxi runs in Pattaya — Pattaya Mail). Set "Hilton Pattaya" as your destination.

For getting back, book a ride-hailing cab again or pick up a taxi from the hotel lobby.
