# Skybar Summer Club — 27th-floor rooftop bar in Pattaya

## intro

Swim in the double-decker pool during the day, then stay on as the place turns into a night venue with live DJ music after sunset. That is Skybar Summer Club, on the 27th floor — the top floor — of Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya. Its official website presents it as Pattaya's highest rooftop bar and the city's only double-decker pool venue.

Come in the morning or afternoon and it is a relaxed poolside affair — sun loungers, swimming, an easy pace. As the sun begins to dip, it is cocktail time against the sunset; once night falls, the music takes over with live DJ sets, pole-art performances, ice-carving shows and outdoor movie screenings. The real charm of the bar is how completely different it feels depending on when you visit.

It is an adults-only space: entry is 18 and above. It suits groups of friends who want to hang out and chat, anyone looking for a party vibe, and those who want DJ music running late into the night. It is not the place for quiet dinners or families with kids — the official website itself directs families to the BARBÙ pool on the 24th floor of the same hotel.

## facts

- **Location**: 27th floor of Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya (hotel's top floor)
- **Address**: 390 Thanon Pattaya Sai 2, Pattaya City, Bang Lamung District, Chon Buri 20150, Thailand
- **Hours**: Daily 9:00 AM – midnight (official FAQ, 'Open daily')
- **Entry**: 18+ only. Non-guests pay 500 THB per person, redeemable as a food-and-drink voucher — more a prepayment than an admission fee. In-house guests enter free.
- **Happy hour**: Runs daily. Note the timings differ across sources — the main page says 6:00–8:00 PM, the FAQ says 6:00–7:00 PM. Worth keeping in mind if you are planning around it.
- **Menu & prices** (official Food/Drink menu PDFs, in baht): cocktails 360–390, non-alcoholic 240–250, beer 170, wine by the glass 230–320, coffee 120–140, snacks around 100–320. Plus 7% VAT and 10% service charge on everything.
- **Reservations**: online tickets (shop.siamatsiam.com), LINE @siamatsiampattaya, WhatsApp +66 93 959 2582, phone +66 (0) 38 930 600, email rsvn@siamatpattaya.com. Private parties, corporate events, birthdays and group bookings go through the event enquiry page.
- **Phone**: +66 (0) 38 930 600

## reasons

The appeal is getting two completely different days out of one spot. Mornings and afternoons are for the poolside — grab a sun lounger, swim, take it slow. Sunset is for a cocktail against the sky. Night belongs to the music: live DJ sets, pole-art performances, ice-carving shows and outdoor movies, with the official website urging guests to "come early for the sunset and stay for the DJ sets as the night deepens."

It suits friends who want music and a long night out together. It is not for quiet dinners or family visits with kids — the officially suggested alternative for families is BARBÙ on the 24th floor.

## access

- **Getting there**: North Pattaya, around Pattaya Second Road (Sai Song). The official factsheet places it 350 m from Pattaya Beach.
- **Transit**: Pattaya has no BTS or MRT, so songthaews (shared trucks) and taxis are the basics.
- **Grab·Bolt**: search "Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya" or "Skybar Summer Club" in the app. The official FAQ's "Click to see map" link also helps with directions.
- **Map**: 12.9457203 / 100.8887009
- **Getting home**: it runs till midnight, so book a Grab or Bolt in the app or take a taxi from the hotel entrance.

## faq

**What are the hours?**
Daily 9:00 AM to midnight. The official FAQ says 'Open daily', so there are no closing days.

**Can non-guests enter?**
Yes — walk-ins are officially welcome. Non-guests pay 500 THB per person, converted into a food-and-drink voucher. In-house guests enter free.

**Can I bring kids?**
Entry is 18+ only. For families, the officially suggested alternative is the BARBÙ pool on the 24th floor.

**Is there a happy hour?**
Daily. Just note the listed timings differ — 6:00–8:00 PM on the main page versus 6:00–7:00 PM in the FAQ. Keep that in mind while planning.

**What about the menu and prices?**
The official Food/Drink menu PDFs list prices in baht: cocktails 360–390, non-alcoholic 240–250, beer 170, wine by the glass 230–320, coffee 120–140, snacks around 100–320. Plus 7% VAT and 10% service charge.

**How do I book?**
Online tickets (shop.siamatsiam.com), official LINE @siamatsiampattaya, WhatsApp +66 93 959 2582, phone +66 (0) 38 930 600, or email rsvn@siamatpattaya.com.

**Is the pool under renovation?**
Booking platforms carried a pool renovation notice for 25 Aug–25 Sep 2026. The official website now promotes daily operation and pool use as normal. If the pool is the reason for your visit, call +66 (0) 38 930 600 and ask about pool status before you go.

**How do I get back?**
It runs till midnight, so book a Grab or Bolt in the app or take a taxi from the hotel entrance. Search "Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya" as the destination.
