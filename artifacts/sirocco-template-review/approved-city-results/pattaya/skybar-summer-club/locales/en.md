# Skybar Summer Club — 27th-floor rooftop bar in Pattaya

## intro

Swim in the double-decker pool by day, then stay as the place turns into a night venue with live DJ sound after sunset. That's Skybar Summer Club, on the 27th floor — the top floor — of Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya. Its official site presents it as Pattaya's highest rooftop bar and the city's only double-decker pool venue.

Come in the morning or afternoon and it's a laid-back poolside stretch — sun loungers, swimming, easy hours. As the sun starts to drop, it becomes cocktail time against the sunset; once night falls, the music takes over with live DJ sets, pole-art performances, ice-carving shows, and outdoor movie screenings. The bar's real charm is how completely different it feels depending on when you visit.

It's an adults-only space: entry is 18 and over. It's a great fit for groups of friends who want to hang out and talk, anyone after a party atmosphere, and people looking for DJ sound that runs late into the night. It's not the place for quiet dinners or families with kids — the official site itself points families to the BARBÙ pool on the 24th floor of the same hotel.

## facts

- **Location**: 27th floor of Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya (hotel's top floor)
- **Address**: 390 Thanon Pattaya Sai 2, Pattaya City, Bang Lamung District, Chon Buri 20150, Thailand
- **Hours**: Daily 9:00 AM – midnight (official FAQ, 'Open daily')
- **Entry**: 18+ only. Non-guests pay 500 THB per person, redeemable as a food-and-drink voucher — closer to a prepayment than an admission fee. In-house guests enter free.
- **Happy hour**: Runs daily. Note the times differ between sources — the main page says 6:00–8:00 PM, the FAQ says 6:00–7:00 PM. Worth knowing if you're planning around it.
- **Menu & prices** (official Food/Drink menu PDFs, in baht): cocktails 360–390, non-alcoholic 240–250, beer 170, wine by the glass 230–320, coffee 120–140, snacks around 100–320. Plus 7% VAT and 10% service charge on everything.
- **Reservations**: online tickets (shop.siamatsiam.com), LINE @siamatsiampattaya, WhatsApp +66 93 959 2582, phone +66 (0) 38 930 600, email rsvn@siamatpattaya.com. Private parties, corporate events, birthdays, and group bookings go through the event enquiry page.
- **Phone**: +66 (0) 38 930 600

## reasons

The appeal is getting two completely different days out of one spot. Mornings and afternoons are for the poolside — grab a sun lounger, swim, take it slow. Sunset is for a cocktail against the sky. Night belongs to the music: live DJ sets, pole-art performances, ice-carving shows, and outdoor movies, with the official site urging guests to "come early for the sunset and stay for the DJ sets as the night deepens."

It suits friends who want music and a late night out together. It's not for quiet dinners or family visits with kids — for families, the officially suggested alternative is BARBÙ on the 24th floor.

## access

- **Getting there**: North Pattaya, around Pattaya Second Road (Sai Song). The official factsheet puts it 350 m from Pattaya Beach.
- **Transit**: Pattaya has no BTS or MRT, so songthaews (shared trucks) and taxis are the basics.
- **Grab·Bolt**: search "Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya" or "Skybar Summer Club" in the app. The official FAQ's "Click to see map" link helps with directions too.
- **Map**: 12.9457203 / 100.8887009
- **Getting home**: it runs until midnight, so call a Grab or Bolt in the app or catch a taxi at the hotel entrance.

## faq

**What are the hours?**
Daily 9:00 AM to midnight. The official FAQ says 'Open daily', so there are no closing days.

**Can non-guests enter?**
Yes — walk-ins are officially welcome. Non-guests pay 500 THB per person, converted into a food-and-drink voucher. In-house guests enter free.

**Can I bring kids?**
Entry is 18+ only. For families, the officially suggested alternative is the BARBÙ pool on the 24th floor.

**Is there a happy hour?**
Daily. Just note the listed times differ — 6:00–8:00 PM on the main page versus 6:00–7:00 PM in the FAQ. Keep that in mind when planning.

**What about the menu and prices?**
The official Food/Drink menu PDFs list prices in baht: cocktails 360–390, non-alcoholic 240–250, beer 170, wine by the glass 230–320, coffee 120–140, snacks around 100–320. Plus 7% VAT and 10% service charge.

**How do I book?**
Online tickets (shop.siamatsiam.com), official LINE @siamatsiampattaya, WhatsApp +66 93 959 2582, phone +66 (0) 38 930 600, or email rsvn@siamatpattaya.com.

**Is the pool under renovation?**
Booking platforms carried a pool renovation notice for 25 Aug–25 Sep 2026. The official site now promotes daily operation and pool use as normal. If the pool is the point of your visit, call +66 (0) 38 930 600 and ask about pool status before you go.

**How do I get home?**
It runs until midnight, so call a Grab or Bolt in the app or take a taxi from the hotel entrance. Search "Siam@Siam Design Hotel Pattaya" as the destination.
