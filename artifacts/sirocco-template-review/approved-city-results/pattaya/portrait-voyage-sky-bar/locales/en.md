# The Portrait of Voyage Sky Bar

## intro

On the 33rd floor of Grande Centre Point Voyage Pattaya, this is an open-air rooftop bar built around cocktails. The hotel's official site introduces it as "Rooftop Bar / Cocktails," and the concept is a "living portrait" — the sky shifts from gold to pink to deep dusk as the sun goes down, a different scene every day.

By day you take in the sea and the city of Pattaya at a glance; at sunset you watch the sun drop; at night the city lights take over, so the mood changes with the hour. It's open daily from 11:30 to midnight (per the hotel), so arriving about an hour before sunset lets you ride the whole arc from sunset into night.

The same 33rd floor also holds Blue Atlantis Rooftop Restaurant, a separate venue focused on dining. This one is a cocktail bar, so plan it as drinks-with-a-view time rather than dinner.

## facts

- Location: 33rd floor, Grande Centre Point Voyage Pattaya (top floor)
- Hours: Daily 11:30–24:00 (per the hotel's official site)
- Address: 808, 908 M.5 Na Kluea, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150, Thailand
- Phone: +66 33 268 188 (hotel main line)
- Coordinates: 12.9536553, 100.8861868 (building location)

## reasons

**A sunset that changes every day.** True to its "living portrait" concept, the sky turns from gold to pink to dusk while you look down over the skyline and the ocean from the 33rd-floor terrace. The daytime sea and the nighttime city lights give each hour a different face.

**The ease of a cocktail bar.** This isn't a dining restaurant — it's about drinks with a view. It works well as a one- or two-hour breather in the middle of a packed sightseeing day.

**Not the same as Blue Atlantis.** Same 33rd floor, different venues: Blue Atlantis is dining-focused, this one is cocktail-focused. Head to Blue Atlantis for a proper dinner; come here for a light drink and the view.

## access

Pattaya has no BTS or MRT, so songthaews (shared trucks), taxis, and ride-hailing apps are the standard way around. The hotel publishes no bar-specific transit directions.

In the Grab or Bolt app, search for "Grande Centre Point Voyage Pattaya" as your destination. The hotel sits in the Na Kluea (North Pattaya) area.
