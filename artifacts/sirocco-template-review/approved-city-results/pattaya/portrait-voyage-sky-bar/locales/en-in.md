# The Portrait of Voyage Sky Bar

## intro

On the 33rd floor of Grande Centre Point Voyage Pattaya, this is an open-air rooftop bar centred on cocktails. The hotel's official site introduces it as "Rooftop Bar / Cocktails," and the concept is a "living portrait" — the sky changes from gold to pink to deep dusk as the sun goes down, a different scene every day.

In the daytime you can see the sea and the city of Pattaya in one glance; at sunset you watch the sun set; at night the city lights take over, so the mood keeps changing with the hour. It is open daily from 11:30 to midnight (as per the hotel), so reaching about an hour before sunset lets you enjoy the full stretch from sunset into night.

The same 33rd floor also has Blue Atlantis Rooftop Restaurant, a separate venue focused on dining. This one is a cocktail bar, so plan it as drinks-with-a-view time rather than dinner.

## facts

- Location: 33rd floor, Grande Centre Point Voyage Pattaya (top floor)
- Hours: Daily 11:30–24:00 (as per the hotel's official site)

## reasons

**A sunset that changes every day.** True to its "living portrait" concept, the sky turns from gold to pink to dusk while you look down at the skyline and the ocean from the 33rd-floor terrace. The daytime sea and the nighttime city lights give every hour a different feel.

**The relaxed charm of a cocktail bar.** This is not a dining restaurant — it is about drinks with a view. It works nicely as a one- or two-hour break in the middle of a busy sightseeing day.

**Not the same as Blue Atlantis.** Same 33rd floor, different venues: Blue Atlantis is dining-focused, this one is cocktail-focused. Go to Blue Atlantis for a proper dinner; come here for a light drink and the view.

## access

Pattaya has no BTS or MRT, so songthaews (shared trucks), taxis and ride-hailing apps are the usual way to get around. The hotel has not published bar-specific public transport directions.

In the Grab or Bolt app, search for "Grande Centre Point Voyage Pattaya" as your destination. The hotel is in the Na Kluea (North Pattaya) area.
