# The Portrait of Voyage Sky Bar

## intro

Up on the 33rd floor of Grande Centre Point Voyage Pattaya, this is an open-air rooftop bar centered on cocktails. The hotel's official site bills it as "Rooftop Bar / Cocktails," and the concept is a "living portrait" — the sky shifts from gold to pink to deep dusk as the sun sets, a different scene every single day.

During the day you get the Pattaya sea and city in one sweeping look; at sunset you watch the sun go down; at night the city lights take over, so the vibe changes by the hour. Open daily 11:30 AM to midnight (per the hotel), so showing up about an hour before sunset lets you catch the full run from sunset into night.

The same 33rd floor is also home to Blue Atlantis Rooftop Restaurant, a separate venue focused on dining. This spot is a cocktail bar, so think of it as drinks-with-a-view time rather than dinner.

## facts

- Location: 33rd floor, Grande Centre Point Voyage Pattaya (top floor)
- Hours: Daily 11:30 AM–midnight (per the hotel's official site)

## reasons

**A sunset that changes every day.** True to its "living portrait" concept, the sky turns from gold to pink to dusk while you gaze down at the skyline and the ocean from the 33rd-floor terrace. The daytime sea and the nighttime city lights give each hour its own look.

**The laid-back feel of a cocktail bar.** This isn't a dining restaurant — it's about drinks with a view. It's a great one- or two-hour break in the middle of a packed sightseeing day.

**Not the same as Blue Atlantis.** Same 33rd floor, different venues: Blue Atlantis is all about dining, this one is all about cocktails. Go to Blue Atlantis for a real dinner; come here for a light drink and the view.

## access

Pattaya doesn't have a BTS or MRT, so songthaews (shared trucks), taxis, and ride-hailing apps are the standard way to get around. The hotel doesn't publish bar-specific transit directions.

In the Grab or Bolt app, search for "Grande Centre Point Voyage Pattaya" as your destination. The hotel is in the Na Kluea (North Pattaya) area.
