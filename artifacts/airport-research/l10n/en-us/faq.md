# U.S.–Thailand Airport FAQ (for U.S. travelers)

## Is there a nonstop flight from the U.S. to Thailand?

No. As of October 13, 2026, no airline flies nonstop between the United States and Thailand on any airport pair — all 352 verified U.S.–Thailand airport pairs require a connection. Do not book an itinerary that claims a nonstop.

## Which U.S. airline flies to Bangkok?

United Airlines is the only U.S. carrier serving Bangkok (United newsroom, April 2025), and it does not fly nonstop. UA820/821 operates daily between Los Angeles (LAX) and Bangkok (BKK) with a Hong Kong (HKG) stop: UA820 leaves LAX at 23:15 and arrives BKK at 11:25; UA821 leaves BKK at 16:55 and arrives LAX at 20:25 (Tourism Authority of Thailand).

## Does Thai Airways fly from the U.S. to Thailand?

No. Thai Airways ended its Bangkok–Los Angeles service on October 25, 2015, and its Bangkok–New York (JFK) service in 2008. It has no U.S. operation as of October 13, 2026.

## Where do I check in for Thai Airways at Bangkok Suvarnabhumi (BKK)?

International economy class: rows **H6–H17**. Royal Silk: rows **A3–A10**. Self baggage drop: **D5–D9** and **J5–J19**. Check-in opens 8 hours before departure and closes 50 minutes before departure (owner-confirmed from Thai Airways' official airport information page, October 13, 2026).

## How do I get a delay or cancellation certificate from Thai Airways?

Use the official **"Request Flight Disruption"** form on thaiairways.com — it is specifically for passengers who need a document for insurance purposes after a delay, cancellation, or diversion.

## How do I get proof of a United delay for travel insurance?

Email **delayletter@united.com** with the names of everyone in your party, your confirmation number, flight numbers and travel dates, a phone contact, and the email or mailing address where the letter should be sent. Allow 5–10 business days. (This process is reported from United's own delays-and-cancellations page and has not been re-verified by a direct text fetch.)

## My bag was delayed on a United flight. What do I do?

Report it at the destination airport's baggage service desk before you leave. United delivers bags through Bags VIP at 250+ U.S. airports and grants an automatic bag fee refund in some cases when a bag is delayed over 12 hours. If a Bags VIP delivery is disrupted, call **1-877-847-0045**.

## My bag was delayed on a Thai Airways flight. What do I do?

Report to ground staff at the arrival airport immediately and get your report copy plus File Reference Number. File the written claim (PIR plus receipts) within **21 days** of delivery. For Bangkok: **baggage.service@thaiairways.com** or **baggageclaim@thaiairways.com**. International Lost & Found at BKK: **+66-2134-5453~56**, 24 hours.

## Do I have to request a refund for a cancelled flight to the U.S.?

No. Under the U.S. DOT's automatic refund rule, airlines must issue cash refunds automatically when owed — within 7 business days for credit card purchases, 20 days otherwise — and may not substitute vouchers unless you affirmatively accept them. This covers cancellations and significant changes (international flights delayed 6+ hours, airport changes, added connections, downgrades).

## Can I get cash compensation for a delayed flight under U.S. rules?

No general U.S. rule provides cash compensation beyond refunds. Your travel insurance policy is the main source of delay reimbursement — which is why the airline's delay letter matters.

## What documents does a U.S. travel insurer want after a flight delay?

Typically: written proof of the delay (the airline's letter or certificate), your itinerary and booking confirmation (PNR), boarding passes, itemized receipts for reasonable expenses, and time-stamped photos where relevant. Check your own policy's claim checklist — terms and state variations apply.

## Where do I get airport help at Suvarnabhumi?

The Airports of Thailand Contact Center is **1722** (24 hours); the airport's main line is **+66 2 132 1888**. AOT does not publish an airline-by-airline counter directory — for counter questions, use the airline's own staff or the flight information boards.

## Where does Thai Airways sell tickets at BKK?

Ticketing changes are directed to the THAI office in your departure city. The THAI Shop at Suvarnabhumi (OPC Center, tel 02 137 0175) is a souvenir shop, not a ticketing counter. General reservations: THAI Customer Contact Center **+66 2 356-1111**.
