# India–Thailand Flights: Frequently Asked Questions

*Answers for Indian travellers flying to Thailand. Sources: airline official sites, airport operator (AOT), and Indian government authorities. Last verified: 13 October 2026.*

## Routes and airlines

**Which Indian cities have direct flights to Thailand?**
To Bangkok Suvarnabhumi (BKK): Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Bhubaneswar, Ahmedabad — plus Gaya and Guwahati on IndiGo from late October 2026. To Bangkok Don Mueang (DMK): Delhi and Amritsar — plus Gaya, Ahmedabad, Guwahati, Hyderabad, Lucknow and Jaipur on Thai AirAsia from late October/November 2026. To Phuket (HKT): Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru. To Krabi (KBV): Delhi. Only services confirmed by official airline, airport, or government sources are listed here.

**Which airlines fly non-stop between India and Thailand?**
IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express, SpiceJet (currently discontinued services), Thai Airways, Thai Vietjet Air, Thai AirAsia, Thai AirAsia X, Thai Lion Air. Nok Air's international services have been suspended since August 2025 with no resumption.

**Is my flight to BKK or DMK?**
These are two different Bangkok airports — Suvarnabhumi (BKK) and Don Mueang (DMK). Always confirm the airport code on your booking before leaving for the airport.

## Check-in and counters

**Where do I check in at Suvarnabhumi (BKK)?**
International departures check in on Level 4. Thai Airways uses counters H6–H17 (economy), A3–A10 (Royal Silk/business), D5–D9 / J5–J19 (online baggage drop) — opening 8 hours before departure, closing 50 minutes before; checked baggage at least 60 minutes before departure. For other India–Thailand carriers, no fixed counter rows are published — check the flight information displays and your boarding pass on the day.

**Where do I check in for Thai AirAsia X at Don Mueang?**
Terminal 1 (international terminal), Level 3, counters 6–7, opening 3 hours before departure.

**Can I buy a ticket at the airport counter in Bangkok?**
Generally no — India–Thailand carriers sell tickets online or via their apps. Day-of-travel changes go through the airline's service desk in the departures hall. Thai AirAsia and Thai AirAsia X publish no ticket-sales counter locations inside DMK.

**When should I arrive at the airport?**
Check-in counters typically open 3 hours before departure and close about an hour before. For Thai Airways international flights at BKK, check-in closes 60 minutes before departure.

## Baggage problems (PIR)

**My bag is delayed or lost. What do I do first?**
Report it to the airline's ground staff at the arrival airport **before leaving the arrivals hall** and file a Property Irregularity Report (PIR). Keep the PIR reference number, baggage tag, boarding pass and receipts for essential purchases.

**What is the deadline for a delayed-baggage claim?**
IndiGo's official Conditions of Carriage require notification within 21 days from the date the bag is placed at your disposal. Thai Airways requires a written claim within 21 days of delivery.

**Where is the baggage service desk at Phuket airport?**
In the international arrivals hall on Level 1: Thai Airways near baggage belt No. 5, and AOTGA near belt No. 1 (per the AOT official arrival procedure).

**Will I be reimbursed for toiletries I bought while my bag was delayed?**
Keep all original receipts and submit them with your claim — airlines reimburse reasonable essential expenses under their carriage terms.

## Delay and cancellation certificates

**How do I get a delay certificate from IndiGo?**
Use IndiGo's official online Travel Certificate portal with your PNR/booking reference and email. Certificates are charged per passenger per sector; records older than 18 months go through the 24/7 call centre.

**How do I get a delay certificate from Thai Airways / Air India / others?**
No official online certificate procedure was found on official channels for these carriers as of 13 October 2026. Ask the airline's ground staff at the departure airport for written confirmation of the disruption at the time, and keep your boarding pass, booking reference and delay notifications.

**Do I need a certificate for an airline refund?**
No — refunds go through the airline's normal process. Certificates matter mainly for insurance claims and expense claims.

## Passenger rights in India (DGCA)

**What are my rights if my India–Thailand flight is delayed or cancelled?**
Under DGCA's CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV (mirrored in the Passenger Charter), which covers flights to, from and within India on any airline's nationality: on cancellation, an alternate flight or compensation plus a full refund, and meals if you have already reported at the airport; on delay, meals and refreshments, and depending on length an alternate flight or full refund, or hotel accommodation with transfers. No compensation is owed for force-majeure disruptions beyond the airline's control.

**Where do I complain if the airline does not follow the rules?**
First to the airline, then on the AirSewa app or portal, then to statutory bodies or courts if unresolved.

## Indian travel insurance claims

**What documents do Indian insurers typically ask for?**
Per IRDAI-filed travel policy wordings, commonly: the completed and signed claim form; the original ticket/boarding pass or passport copy showing travel dates; all original bills, vouchers and receipts; and for baggage-delay claims, the airline's PIR. Medical claims need doctor's notes, admission/discharge cards, itemised bills with prescriptions and diagnostic reports, plus passport/visa copies with entry/exit stamps. Passport loss needs an FIR/final police report. Your insurer's own policy wording prevails.

**Does filing a PIR guarantee my insurance payout?**
No. The PIR proves the event happened and is usually required, but the insurer decides the claim under your policy's terms, exclusions and time limits.
