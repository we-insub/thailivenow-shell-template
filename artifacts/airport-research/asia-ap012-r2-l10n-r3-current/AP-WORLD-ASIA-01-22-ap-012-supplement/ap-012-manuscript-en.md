# ap-012 Airline Airport Services Guide (MY↔TH / IN↔TH, Counterpart Airports) — English

> Verified: 2026-10-14. All items are based on official airline/airport sources. Unverified items may change by season — please check with the airline before departure. BKK/DMK (SERVICE-01) and Thai regional airports (REGIONAL) are handled separately and not included here.

## AK (AirAsia) × KUL

- **Baggage incidents (official)**: Report damage before leaving the airport. For delay, report to Guest Services immediately when bags stop arriving on the carousel — present your booking reference and bag tag to receive a PIR (Property Irregularity Report) form and number. Declare loss within 7 days (domestic) / 14 days (international) from PIR date; notify damage within 7 days of receipt and delay within 21 days in writing.
- **KUL baggage tracing office (official)**: Central Baggage Tracing Office, Level 2, Terminal KLIA2, KL International Airport, Jalan KLIA2, 64000 KLIA, Selangor. Tel +603 8778 5495 / +603 8778 5494, email kulak_baggageservices@groundteamred.com
- **Insurance claims (official)**: Via Tune Protect claims portal (claims.tuneprotect.com) — verify COI → Claim → enter booking number → fill form → upload documents (itinerary, booking confirmation, airline delay certificate, etc.). Enquiries: enquiry@tuneprotect.com, emergency +603 7628 3630 (24h).
- **Unverified**: KUL check-in counter numbers, airport office, delay/cancellation certificate issuance — not published officially. Please check with the airline before departure.
- **Phone**: No official phone line — AskBo chat is the official channel (reused from ap-011).

## FD (Thai AirAsia) × KUL·PEN·AMD·GAU·GAY·HYD·JAI·LKO

Loss declaration: 7 days domestic / 14 days international.
- **Insurance claims (official)**: Same Tune Protect portal process (see AK above).
