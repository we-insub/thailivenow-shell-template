# IndiGo (6E) — BKK Passenger FAQ

## Q1: How do I find my check-in counter at BKK on the day of travel?

IndiGo's official airport directory (https://www.goindigo.in/airport-directory/thailand/bangkok-bkk.html) does not publish BKK-specific counter numbers. Check the airport departure displays or IndiGo's official channels on the day of travel. Counter assignments can change by season.

Note: IndiGo's terminal-information pages (e.g., CSMIA ticketing list showing Mumbai T2 Departure Gate 7 opposite ticketing) are Mumbai-specific and do not apply to BKK. Check-in display information is separate.

## Q2: My baggage was damaged or delayed on arrival. Where do I report it?

Report to the airline staff in the destination arrival hall before you exit. Ask them to create a record and explain the next steps. (Per IndiGo's lost/mishandled baggage guidance.)

The Thailand representative listed on IndiGo's international reservation offices page handles reservations and city-office roles — it is not an arrival PIR contact.

## Q3: I need proof of delay for insurance. Is the Travel Certificate the right document?

No. IndiGo's paid Travel Certificate (travel-certificate.html) is proof of travel, not a disruption certificate. It is a different document from delay/cancellation proof. For insurance claims, confirm with IndiGo's official channels which document your insurer requires, and note that insurer claim deadlines are separate from airline notification deadlines.
